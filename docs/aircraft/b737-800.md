# Boeing 737-800 (winglets, CFM56-7B26): research dossier

This dossier is the contract for the cockpit, exterior and verification agents. Every number is
cited. **EST** marks an estimate and gives its reasoning. **SCOPE** marks a simplification.

Code lives in `src/aircraft/b737-800/`:
- `data.ts`: the numbers
- `fdm.ts`
- `vars.ts`: the control vars and light outputs
- `systems/*`
- `createSystems.ts`, `states.ts`, `checklists.ts`, `inputMap.ts`, `meta.ts`

Tests are in `tests/aircraft/b737-800/`.

Not written by this agent: `cockpit/**`, `exterior.ts` and `index.ts`. Later agents own them.

The avionics suite (six-DU CDS, FMC and CDUs, MCP / AFDS / autothrottle, EFIS panels) is the shared
family `src/avionics/boeing-737` (docs `docs/modules/avionics-boeing-737.md`). This aircraft
configures it in `systems/avionics.ts`.

## 1. General

- Twin-engine, narrow-body, low-wing transport with blended winglets (Aviation Partners Boeing),
  certified under 14 CFR Part 25. Crew of 2. 162 seats in two classes, 189 maximum
  (ACAPS 2.4.13 / TCDS).
- ICAO type designator B738 (Doc 8643).
- Engines: 2 x CFM56-7B26, 26,300 lbf SLST (E004, ACAPS).
- Avionics: Honeywell/Smiths Common Display System with six 8 in DUs, Smiths/GE FMC with two
  CDUs, Collins FCC-A/B driving the AFDS, Honeywell ADIRS, and an ISFD.

## 6. Airframe systems

### 6.1 Electrical (FCOM 6.10/6.20, LIM; `systems/electrical.ts`)

- **Sources.** Two 90 kVA IDGs, the APU generator (90 kVA) and external power, all 115 V / 400 Hz.
  AC limits are 110-120 V and 390-410 Hz. DC is 26 V (22-30 V). The battery is a 24 V NiCd
  (LIM lists 40 Ah; the library preset is 48 Ah). Sources never parallel: each AC transfer bus
  has exactly one source. The source latch (`ac.b738.xfr{1,2}_src`) takes these values:
  0 none, 1 own IDG, 2 APU, 3 ground power, 4 opposite IDG (bus transfer).
  - GEN 1/2 ON connects its IDG through GCB.
  - APU GEN 1/2 ON connects the APU through APB, the tie bus and BTB. Selecting the IDG trips the
    APU off that bus.
  - GRD PWR ON connects the GPU through GPC. It needs `ac.b738.gpu_connected`, and the GRD POWER
    AVAILABLE light shows it.
  - BUS TRANSFER AUTO moves a transfer bus to the opposite IDG when its own generator drops,
    and back when it returns.
  - The fire handle and the drive disconnect open the IDG.
- **Buses and loads.** Breaker ids are `cb.<load>`; the powered flag is `elec.<load>_powered`.

  | Bus | Loads |
  |---|---|
  | AC STBY | DU capt outboard / inboard / upper, DEU1, IRS L (AC), NAV1, left igniters, standby yaw damper |
  | XFR 1 | FMC, CDU1, ADF1, APU SCU, EEC1 alternate power, equipment cooling, fuel CTR L / L FWD / R FWD, HYD ELEC 1 (B), standby hydraulic pump, right igniters, retractable landing lights, anti-collision, position lights, probe heat A, pack control L, recirc fan L, trim air, window heat L SIDE / R FWD, wing lights, wiper L, taxi light |
  | XFR 2 | CDU2, DEU2, DU F/O inboard / outboard, DU lower, EEC2 alternate power, fixed landing lights, fuel CTR R / L AFT / R AFT, HYD ELEC 2 (A), IRS R (AC), NAV2, ADF2, logo, pack control R, probe heat B, recirc fan R, strobes, TCAS, turnoff lights, wheel well light, window heat L FWD / R SIDE, wiper R, weather radar |
  | MAIN 1 / MAIN 2 (CAB/UTIL) | forward galley, IFE; aft galley, cabin lights. Shed in flight on a single generator (FCOM 6.20) |
  | DC 1 (TR1) | annunciator lights, antiskid, A/T, autobrake, bleed control L, cargo fire, eng 1 start valve, eng 1 anti-ice, FCC A, FLT CTL A, fuel valves, gear control, GPS1, Mach warning, MCP 1, panel lights, pressurization AUTO, RA1, SMYD1, stab trim, TAWS, wing anti-ice valves, WXR control, XPDR 2 |
  | DC 2 (TR2 / TR3) | bleed control R, COM2, EFIS2, eng 2 start valve, eng 2 anti-ice, FCC B, flood lights, FLT CTL B, GPS2, MCP 2, RA2, SMYD2, spoiler control, stall warning 2, nosewheel steering, XPDR 1 |
  | DC STBY | alternate flaps, COM1, EFIS1, emergency lights, engine indication, fire detection, DC ignition, ISFD, pressurization ALTN, stall warning 1, standby rudder |
  | BATTERY BUS | APU ECU, APU start (when XFR 1 is unpowered), fire extinguisher, dome light |
  | HOT BATTERY | clock, fire bottle squibs, parking brake valve, passenger oxygen |
  | SW HOT BATTERY | IRS DC backup |

- **TRUs.** TR1 feeds DC 1 from XFR 1. TR2 feeds DC 2 from XFR 2. TR3 feeds DC 2 and the battery
  bus from XFR 2, with XFR 1 as alternate. The cross bus tie joins DC 1 and DC 2; it opens with
  BUS TRANSFER OFF and in a dual-channel approach.
- **Standby power.** With STANDBY POWER AUTO and all AC lost, AC STBY moves to the static
  inverter and DC STBY to the battery bus. BAT forces battery power. OFF gives STANDBY PWR OFF.
- **Meters.** The DC meter selector (STBY PWR / BAT BUS / BAT / TR1 / TR2 / TR3 / TEST) and the AC
  meter selector (STBY PWR / GRD PWR / GEN1 / APU GEN / GEN2 / INV / TEST) drive
  `ac.b738.dc_volts / dc_amps / ac_volts / ac_amps / ac_hz`.

### 6.2 Fuel (FCOM 12.20; `systems/fuel.ts`)

- Six AC boost pumps: L FWD / L AFT (main 1), R FWD / R AFT (main 2), and CTR L / CTR R.
- Centre pumps deliver a higher pressure than the mains (30 vs 23 psi), so centre fuel is burned
  first.
- The crossfeed valve (DC motor) joins the manifolds. The VALVE OPEN light is dim when open and
  bright in transit.
- The start lever and fire handle close the spar and engine valves. SPAR / ENG VALVE CLOSED lights
  are dim when closed and bright in transit.
- Suction feed from the own main tank below ~25,000 ft (EST).
- The APU feeds from the No. 1 manifold, suction-fed from tank 1 when no pump runs.
- LOW PRESSURE lights: main pumps light also when switched off; centre pumps only when ON.

### 6.3 Hydraulics (FCOM 13.20, LIM, B737ORG; `systems/hydraulic.ts`)

- **System A:** ENG 1 EDP (37 gpm) + ELEC 2 EMDP (6 gpm, XFR 2).
- **System B:** ENG 2 EDP + ELEC 1 EMDP (XFR 1).
- Pressure 3,000 psi normal, 2,800 min, 3,500 relief. LOW PRESSURE lights below 1,300 psi.
- Reservoirs: A 21.6 l, B 31.1 l, standby 13.6 l (EST).
- **Standby system:** 3 gpm pump. It runs on:
  - FLT CONTROL STBY RUD,
  - ALTERNATE FLAPS ARM,
  - automatically on loss of A or B with flaps extended, when airborne or above 60 kt wheel speed.

  It powers the standby rudder, the LE devices (extend only) and both reversers.
- **PTU:** A drives B for autoslats and LE devices when B EDP pressure is lost, airborne with flaps
  1-10.
- **Gear transfer unit:** system B retracts the gear when engine 1 N2 is low in flight.
- The EMDPs need at least 760 kg of fuel in the main tanks for ground operation (LIM); this is a
  crew limitation and is not modelled.

### 6.4 Pneumatics, packs, pressurization (FCOM 2.10-2.40; `systems/airframe.ts`)

- Bleeds 1/2 regulate to ~45 psi. APU bleed is limited to 17,000 ft (LIM).
- **Isolation valve.** CLOSE / AUTO / OPEN. In AUTO it opens when either engine bleed or either
  pack switch is off.
- **Consumers:** wing anti-ice (wai_l / wai_r), engine anti-ice (eai1 / eai2), and packs 1/2
  (OFF / AUTO / HIGH).
- **Zones:** flight deck (pack L) and forward / aft cabin, with temperature selectors covering
  18-30 °C (EST).
- **Starters:** st1 / st2 need 30 psi nominal duct pressure.
- **Pressurization** (library preset PRESS_737NG):
  - FLT ALT and LAND ALT selectors, and the AUTO / ALTN / MAN mode selector.
  - MAN uses the spring-loaded outflow switch (`ac.b738.outflow_cmd`).
  - AUTO without power fails to ALTN, lighting AUTO FAIL and ALTN.
  - Cabin altitude warning horn at 10,000 ft cabin, with ALT HORN CUTOUT.
  - OFF SCHED DESCENT when descending before reaching FLT ALT.

### 6.5 Ice and rain (FCOM 3.20)

- **Window heat:** L SIDE, L FWD, R FWD, R SIDE. ON lights; OVERHEAT via the `fail.b738.win_heat_*`
  failures. The test switch has OVHT and PWR TEST.
- **Probe heat A** (Capt pitot, L elev pitot, L alpha vane, TEMP probe) and **B** (F/O pitot,
  R elev pitot, R alpha vane, AUX pitot). The amber lights show the probe is not heated. TAT test
  is SCOPE: it lights only.
- **Wing anti-ice.** Solenoid-held on the ground; the valves open on the ground only with thrust
  below the take-off setting (EST: the ground logic). It trips OFF at lift-off. VALVE OPEN lights.
- **Engine anti-ice 1/2.** Cowl valve lights. COWL ANTI-ICE over-pressure via failures.
- **Wipers L/R:** PARK / INT / LOW / HIGH.

### 6.6 Fire protection (FCOM 8.20; `systems/airframe.ts createFire`)

- **Engines.** Dual loops with OVHT DET A / NORMAL / B selection.
  - The fire handle unlocks with a fire warning or the override. Pulling it closes the fuel,
    hydraulic and bleed valves and trips the generator.
  - Rotating it discharges the L or R bottle (800 psi charge, EST).
  - The BOTTLE DISCHARGED light shows a bottle below the pressure switch.
- **APU.** Single loop and its own bottle.
- **Wheel well.** Detection only.
- **Cargo (fwd / aft).** DET SELECT, ARM, DISCH and TEST, one shared bottle.
- **Test switches.** Fire TEST FAULT/INOP and OVHT/FIRE; EXTINGUISHER TEST 1/2 lights the squib
  lamps.
- **Bell.** The fire bell is the `master_warning` tone. BELL CUTOUT or the master FIRE WARN lights
  silence it.

### 6.7 Oxygen (FCOM 1.30)

- Crew bottle: 114 ft³ class (3,250 l, 1,850 psi full, EST).
- Passenger oxygen: chemical generators, deployed automatically at a cabin altitude of ~14,000 ft,
  or by PASS OXY ON (PASS OXY ON light).

### 6.8 Gear, brakes, steering (FCOM 14.20)

- **Gear.** Lever UP / OFF / DN. Manual extension handles. Lock override trigger.
  - Three green (gear down) and three red (disagree) lights per panel.
  - Gear warning horn with HORN CUTOUT.
  - PSEU light on the ground.
- **Brakes and autobrake.**
  - Autobrake selector: RTO / OFF / 1 / 2 / 3 / MAX (library preset AUTOBRAKE_737NG).
  - AUTO BRAKE DISARM light.
  - ANTISKID INOP.
  - Parking brake light.
  - Brake accumulator gauge.
- **Nosewheel steering.** Tiller ±78° (EST, FCOM), pedals ±7°. The NWS switch selects ALT, which
  moves steering to system A.

### 6.9 Flight controls (FCOM 9.20)

- **Hydraulic power.** Dual-hydraulic ailerons, elevators and rudder (A and B). FLT CONTROL A/B
  switches: STBY RUD / OFF / ON.
- **Spoilers.** SPOILER A/B switches. Speedbrake lever DOWN / ARMED / FLIGHT DETENT / UP; auto
  speedbrake deploys on landing and RTO.
- **Yaw damper.** Solenoid-held switch.
- **Stabilizer trim.**
  - Main electric trim from the yoke switches (both halves must move).
  - Autopilot trim.
  - Manual trim wheels (`trim.pitch_units`).
  - MAIN ELECT and AUTO PILOT cutout switches.
  - Column cutout, bypassed by STAB TRIM OVERRIDE.
- **Aileron and rudder trim.** Two-switch aileron trim; rudder trim knob.
- **Flaps.** Hydraulic system B, with alternate flaps (ARM + electric UP/DOWN switch). Flap load
  relief retracts 40→30, 30→25 and 25→15 above the placard (FLAP LOAD RELIEF light).
- **Leading-edge devices.**
  - Slats: full EXT from flaps 10.
  - Autoslat: flaps 1-5 near the stall.
  - Lights: LE FLAPS TRANSIT / EXT; the LE devices panel with its test.
- **Speed trim / Mach trim.** SCOPE: the FCC functions show their FAIL lights only. The FDM has
  no speed/Mach trim augmentation.

### 6.10 Avionics, sensors and automatic flight (FCOM 4, 10, 11, 15; `systems/avionics.ts`)

- **ADIRS.**
  - IRS L on AC STBY and IRS R on XFR 2, with DC backup on the switched hot battery bus.
  - Mode selectors `ac.irs{1,2}_mode`: OFF / ALIGN / NAV / ATT. Present position is entered on
    the CDU.
  - Alignment takes ~5-10 min depending on latitude (library Irs).
  - The ISDU display selector and system selector drive `ac.b738.isdu_l/_r`.
- **Air data.** ADC 1 (Capt) and 2 (F/O). The ISFD uses ADC 3 plus its own AHRS (index 3).
- **Other sensors.** RA 1/2 (2,500 ft). GPS 1/2 (`ac.b738.gps_installed`).
- **Avionics suite** (`createB737Suite`, src/avionics/boeing-737):
  - CDS: 6 DUs (PFD / ND / upper and lower EICAS), DEU 1/2, EFIS control panels, display
    select, SOURCE / CONTROL PANEL transfer.
  - FMC with two CDUs.
  - MCP: CMD A/B, CWS A/B, FD, A/T ARM, N1, SPEED, VNAV, LNAV, LVL CHG, HDG SEL, APP, VOR/LOC,
    ALT HLD, V/S.
  - AFDS with dual-channel APP (LAND 3 / FLARE / ROLLOUT). ROLLOUT is added through the afcs
    override (see Open issues).
  - Autothrottle: N1, MCP SPD, FMC SPD, RETARD, THR HLD, ARM.
  - Radios: VHF NAV, ADF, marker.
  - The suite lists its display ids through `sys.suite.displays`.
- **Warnings.**
  - Stall warning: two SMYDs driving the stick shaker (tested with STALL WARNING TEST 1/2).
  - Overspeed clacker at Vmo/Mmo (tested with MACH AIRSPEED WARNING TEST 1/2).
  - Altitude alert: 750 / 200 ft.
  - EGPWS class A, with FLAP / GEAR / TERR INHIBIT, SYS TEST and BELOW G/S.
  - TCAS II from the ATC/TCAS panel.

## 8. Normal procedures (`checklists.ts`, `states.ts`)

The cold & dark to engine start flow is verified by `start.test.ts`:
1. BAT ON (guard closed) and STANDBY POWER AUTO. The battery bus, DC STBY and AC STBY (inverter)
   become powered; TRANSFER BUS OFF lights.
2. IRS selectors to NAV.
3. APU: switch to START, then release to ON. The APU is suction-fed from tank 1; the inlet door
   opens, and the start takes 30-120 s (LIM). APU GEN 1 and 2 ON.
4. Enter present position on the CDU (`irs.pos_entry`).
5. Fuel pumps (mains) ON; hydraulic pumps ON; APU BLEED ON; packs OFF; isolation valve AUTO.
6. Start engine 2, then engine 1:
   - ENGINE START switch to GRD.
   - At 25 % N2, start lever to IDLE (fuel on).
   - The starter cuts out at 56 % N2 and the switch releases to OFF.
   - EGT stays below 725 °C.
7. GEN 1 and 2 ON; APU BLEED OFF; packs AUTO; APU OFF.

Checklists follow the QRH normal checklist sequence: BEFORE START, BEFORE TAXI, BEFORE TAKEOFF,
AFTER TAKEOFF, DESCENT, APPROACH, LANDING, SHUTDOWN, SECURE. Items are checked live against the vars.

State presets (`applyB738State`):

| Preset | Configuration |
|---|---|
| `cold_dark` | Everything off; parking brake set; doors closed |
| `ready_to_taxi` | Engines idle, generators on, IRS aligned, CONT ignition, RTO autobrake, flaps 5, probe heat ON, stab 5 units |
| `takeoff` | Lined up: flaps 5, A/T ARM, FD on, N1 limit TO |
| `cruise` | Trimmed at the given level, CMD A in HDG SEL / ALT HOLD, A/T MCP SPD. CMD A engages a few seconds after the load, once air data is valid |
| `approach` | Gear down, flaps 15 (EST), trimmed, CMD A |

### 8.1 Key non-normal procedures (QRH titles; behaviour verified in `failures.test.ts`)

- **ENGINE FIRE or ENGINE SEVERE DAMAGE or SEPARATION:**
  1. A/T disengage; thrust lever close.
  2. Start lever CUTOFF.
  3. Fire handle pull.
  4. Rotate to discharge; after 30 s rotate to the other bottle if still lit.
- **Loss of one IDG (SOURCE OFF, TRANSFER BUS OFF briefly):** BUS TRANSFER AUTO restores the bus
  from the other IDG. APU GEN ON if needed. DRIVE DISCONNECT if the DRIVE light is on (guarded,
  irreversible in flight).
- **Loss of both IDGs:** STANDBY POWER AUTO puts the standby buses on the battery (~30 min battery
  life). Start the APU.
- **Loss of system A or B (LOW PRESSURE lights):** the other system and the autopilot on the
  remaining channel keep flying. Alternate flaps if B is lost (standby pump, ARM + DOWN).
- **Window overheat, pack trip, bleed trip:** OVERHEAT / PACK / BLEED TRIP OFF lights; TRIP RESET
  after cooling.
- **Reverser unlocked, cabin altitude warning (horn, ALT HORN CUTOUT, oxygen masks), gear
  disagree / PSEU.**

## 9. Simulation model summary and verification

`createSystems.ts` builds the update order:
- failures → AC source latch → early logic
- electrical → APU → fuel → hydraulics → pneumatics → pressurization → ice → fire → oxygen
- IRS / ADC / ISFD AHRS / RA → gear → thrust ratings → 737 avionics suite
- EEC levers → start controllers → yaw damper → stall warning → flight controls / trims / flaps /
  speedbrakes / steering / brakes
- overspeed → altitude alert → EGPWS → TCAS → TOCW → CasManager → disconnect aurals → lighting
  → late logic

Every control var in `b738ControlVars()` is read by at least one system (`inventory.test.ts`).

| Test | What it checks |
|---|---|
| (a) `start.test.ts` | cold & dark start |
| (b) `takeoff.test.ts` | take-off distance within 15 %; TO/GA on the ground (A/T N1 → take-off N1, THR HLD at 84 kt); TOCW horn |
| (c) `performance.test.ts` | climb, and cruise TAS / fuel flow within 8 % |
| (d) `performance.test.ts` | stall speeds within 5 %; stick shaker ahead of the stall |
| (e) `performance.test.ts` | Vmo 340 / Mmo 0.82 clacker |
| (f) `approach.test.ts` | KSFO ILS 28R, captured 14 nm out, 30° intercept |
| (g) `failures.test.ts` | IDG 1 loss; hydraulic A loss; engine 1 fire; both IDGs lost |
| `states.test.ts` | every preset is stable |

Approach result (f): LOC capture at 48 s; max deviation 0.04 dot LOC and 0.26 dot G/S; LAND 3,
FLARE, RETARD and ROLLOUT; touchdown at −58 fpm, 376 m past the threshold; autobrake 2 and
reversers stop the aircraft.

## 10. Cockpit control inventory

Conventions:
- Var names are shown with the `ac.b738.` prefix omitted unless another prefix is given. Use the
  `B738.*` accessors in `vars.ts`.
- Positions are listed in the order the `ToggleSwitch` `positions` / `values` arrays expect.
- The (x, y) positions are panel-local millimetres from the panel's top-left corner. They are EST,
  laid out after published 737NG panel photographs and FCOM panel figures.
- Sizes are EST; standard Boeing toggles are ~12 mm bat handles on 25-30 mm pitch.
- Suite-owned controls (MCP, EFIS panels, display select, CDUs, N1 SET / SPD REF / FUEL FLOW,
  A/P / A/T / FMC disengage lights) are built from `src/avionics/boeing-737/cockpit.ts` helpers
  and are listed only by name here.

### 10.0 Panel geometry

Offsets are measured from the Captain design eye `EYE_CAPT` = body [13.754, −0.53, −0.42] m, and
given as (forward, right, down).

## 11. Scope notes

- **Weather radar:** control state only; there are no returns.
- **Audio control panels:** selection state only; there is no audio routing.
- **Test functions that only light annunciators:** TAT TEST, NAV TEST, the zone OVHT TEST and the
  CVR test. They drive their lights and the logic but have no BITE.
- **Speed trim and Mach trim:** annunciators only. The FDM has no speed-stability augmentation.
- **EEC ALTN:** changes the annunciation only. The N1 law is unchanged.
- **Galley and IFE:** electrical loads only.
- **Doors:** open/closed vars and lights only. The exterior agent animates them.
- **Performance figures:** estimated (EST) where §5.1 says so. There is no public AFM performance
  data.
- **Rating selection:** without a navigation database (no FMC), the thrust rating computer selects
  ratings automatically.

### 12.5 Exterior (`exterior.ts`)

- **Anchors.** ACAPS dimensions and the fdm.ts positions, checked by `exterior.test.ts`: length
  39.2-39.8 m, span 35.3-36.3 m, tail height 12.3-12.8 m.
- **Wing.** Planform with the TE kink and 6° dihedral.
- **Moving surfaces.** Double-slotted flaps with Fowler travel, slats, four flight and two ground
  spoilers per wing, ailerons, trimmable stabilizer with elevators, rudder.
- **Winglets and engines.** Blended winglets. CFM56-7 nacelles with the flattened lower lip,
  translating-sleeve reversers and rotating fans.
- **Gear.** The main gear retracts inboard into open wells with no doors; the nose gear retracts
  forward and has doors.
- **Lights.** From `light.*`: position lights including the white aft ones, strobes, beacons,
  fixed and retractable landing lights, turnoff, taxi, logo, wing and wheel-well. The spot
  lights are in candela times `world.render_units_per_lux`.

### 12.6 Scope and estimates (cockpit)

- **Panel geometry.** Positions are EST. The nose is narrower than the dossier's 0.6 m tiller
  offset allows, so the tiller sits 0.35 m outboard of the eye.
- **Controls not built.** SCOPE: they would have no function in the simulation. This covers
  the HF radio control panels, SELCAL, the cabin interphone handset, the F/O tiller (option) and
  chart lights. The ACP receiver controls, R/T - I/C, MASK - BOOM and the wheel MIC / INT switches
  are built; they publish mixer levels and keying state only (no audio routing).
- **Pedestal heights (EST).** The control stand top slopes from the CDU panel's bottom edge
  (x 14.235, z 0.40) up to z 0.22 at the start levers (`layout.ts STAND`, `standTopZ`), so both CDU
  keyboards stay in view above it (a flat top at z 0.30 hid their lower half). The aft electronic
  panel top is at z 0.245, just below the aft end of the stand. The flight-deck floor stays at the
  cabin floor level (z 0.70, 1.12 m below the design eye); with the library seat that leaves the seat
  cushions ~0.65 m below the eye, about 0.13 m less than a typical seated eye height, so the
  panels look slightly low relative to the seats and floor.
- **Fire handle overrides.** They are cockpit-internal mechanical releases (`ac.b738.ck.fire_ovrd_*`).
- **MCP / EFIS panels.** Colours and legends come from the avionics helpers
  (`src/avionics/boeing-737/cockpit.ts`).

Defects found and fixed in this aircraft:
- **APU FAULT on every normal shutdown.** The APU fuel feed followed the APU switch, so APU OFF cut
  the fuel at once and the ECU's 60 s cool-down flamed out (FAULT latched). The feed now follows the ECU
  fuel command (`systems/fuel.ts`).
- **A/T ARM dropped 2 s after it was set in the preflight** from cold & dark: the A/T touchdown
  bookkeeping saw a spurious landing because the air/ground state was not published before the suite
  reset (`states.ts` cold branch now updates the gear first).
- **Stick shaker at V2 on take-off** (flaps 1-5 CLmax too low, see §5.1).
- **Altitude hold hunting ±370 fpm** (12 s limit cycle) at cruise: the ADIRU vertical speed had the
  0.6 s baro-rate lag of a plain ADC; the NG ADIRU uses inertially smoothed vertical speed, now 0.15 s
  (`systems/avionics.ts`): ±66 fpm, ±3 ft.
- **N1 hunting 33-80 % on the glideslope**: A/T speed gain halved (`B738_AT_GAINS`): steady ~57 %.
- **GRD PWR unusable in the app** (no way to connect a GPU): GRD CALL ground crew (§10.4), and the blue
  CALL light now shows incoming calls instead of lighting while the crew's own call buttons are held.
- **PFD FMA hidden under the glareshield** from the design eye: the upper DU row moved 0.08 m down
  (MIP extended downward, `cockpit/layout.ts`); the whole PFD / ND / upper DU is now visible.
- **Render cost**: the 123 breaker white bands are only drawn when a breaker is out (~120 draw calls);
  `verify/drawcalls.test.ts` guards the budget (< 1,850 calls headless; ~2,000 in the app with the
  display quads, 2.2 M triangles, most of them in the library's instanced bezels / collars).

Known defects outside this aircraft's files (reported, not changed; the test works around them the
way a crew would, with a comment at each place):
- `src/avionics/boeing-737/afds/Afds.ts` (midProcess): an MCP altitude equal to the FMC cruise altitude
  is captured as **VNAV ALT** instead of VNAV PTH (FCOM 4.20), and VNAV ALT never starts the descent at
  T/D; pushing VNAV again gives VNAV PTH. Fix: do not set `vnavCapture` when the MCP altitude equals
  the FMC cruise altitude.
- `src/nav/flightplan/FlightPlan.ts` (Boeing style `normalize`): copying the approach's first fix into
  a ROUTE DISCONTINUITY box re-inserts the discontinuity (an IF after another leg always gets a DISCO);
  the crew deletes the discontinuity instead (DEL + LSK, accepted).
- `src/nav/fms/VnavGuidance.ts`: the descent path has no deceleration segment for the 250 kt / 10,000
  ft speed limit or the approach, and the idle path is steep (V/S -4,700 fpm transient at T/D, 280+ kt
  at 11,000 ft); the crew uses SPD INTV 240 kt below 12,000 ft and the speedbrake.
- `src/systems/autopilot/Afcs.ts` (autolandLogic): FMA status turns **NO AUTOLAND** 2 s after touchdown
  because the glideslope goes invalid on the ground; it should keep LAND 3 through the rollout.
- FMA ROLLOUT / FLARE stay on the PFD (F/Ds on) after the A/P is disconnected and the aircraft stopped.
