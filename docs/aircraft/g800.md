# Gulfstream G800 (GVIII-G800): research dossier and module contract

This document is the reference for the G800 aircraft module. It covers the published
data, how each system is modelled, the CAS list, the procedures, and the complete
cockpit control inventory. The cockpit, exterior and `index.ts` agents build from
this document. Every control in the inventory (§9) has a SimVar in
`src/aircraft/g800/vars.ts` (`G800_VARS`) or in the Epic suite
(`src/avionics/honeywell-epic`), and a system reads it. No control is decorative.

Code: `src/aircraft/g800/` contains `data.ts`, `vars.ts`, `fdm.ts`, `systems/*`,
`createSystems.ts`, `states.ts`, `checklists.ts`, `inputMap.ts` and `meta.ts`.
Another agent owns `cockpit/**`, `exterior.ts` and `index.ts`. Tests are in
`tests/aircraft/g800/`: 12 files, 37 tests (including the full-flight check ride in `verify/`), about 95 s.

The G800 is a GVI derivative on type certificate T00015AT. It has the G700 wing
and the G650 fuselage length. Gulfstream publishes very little G800 system
detail. Where the FSB report lists no G800 difference from the G650 (GVI), the
module uses the GVI value and labels it **GVI**. Everything else that is not
published is labelled **EST** and the reasoning is given.

**Model (`fdm.ts`, `systems/engines.ts`).**
- The shared `Turbofan` block. N1 at rated thrust is 91 % EST, idle N1 22 % / N2 60 %, N2 max 99.5 %.
- TSFC 0.0355 lb/lbf/h at rated thrust (EST, calibrated to the Breguet-derived cruise fuel flow in §3.3).
- Idle fuel flow 420 pph. ITT idle 480 °C, max 880 °C, start peak about 620 °C.
- Start model: starter max N2 30 %, light-off at 18 % N2, self-sustaining at 36 %, starter cut-out at 42 %.
- Reverse efficiency 0.4.
- `ThrustLeverFadec` with a linear lever law. Full forward gives the TO rating. The FADEC N1 command is capped by the active rating (`fadec.n1_limit_pct`).
- The ratings table has N1 schedules vs altitude and temperature (EST shape). GA is chosen when airborne with flaps above 25°. CLB is chosen above 1,500 ft RA.
- Approach idle applies with flaps extended.
- EPR (`ac.eng{i}.epr`) is derived from N1 for the Epic engine window. This is an EST mapping.

### 3.2 Other limits

- **Altitudes.** Max 51,000 ft. Max 48,000 ft on a single pack. Flaps 10/20 max 25,000 ft. Flaps 39 and gear extended max 20,000 ft. Max airport elevation 15,000 ft (TCDS, GVI, FSB).
- **Wind and runway.** Takeoff crosswind 30 kt and demonstrated landing crosswind 33 kt, both including gusts. 10 kt crosswind in Alternate, Direct or Backup law. Tailwind 10 kt. Runway slope ±2 % (TCDS §12.2).
- **Temperature.** −50 to +55 °C at SL. Minimum fuel tank temperature −37 °C (GVI, C450).
- **Load factor.** Flaps 0: −1.0 to +2.5 g. Flaps extended: 0 to +2.0 g (GVI).
- **Pressurization.** Max differential 10.69 psid. 0.3 psid for takeoff and landing (GVI).
- **Fuel.** Max imbalance 2,000 lb, 1,000 lb for takeoff. Low level at 650 lb per side (GVI).
- **APU (Honeywell RE220(GVI)).** Max operating altitude 45,000 ft with the generator at 100 % (40 kVA). Starts guaranteed to 37,000 ft. Starter-assisted engine starts at or below 35,000 ft. EGT 1,050 °C start and 732 °C running. Max rotor speed 106 % (TCDS §6, GVI, SCQ).
- **Autopilot.** Min engage height 200 ft AGL. Disengage by 80 ft AGL on ILS/LPV and by 200 ft for other operations (GVI).
- **Ice.** WAI and CAI on at least 2 min before takeoff thrust (GVI; EST for the G800). Auto WAI is inhibited on the ground and above FL350. Auto CAI is inhibited above FL350 (FSB App. 4).
- **Flaps.** Detents UP / 10 / 20 / 39. Takeoff flaps are 10 or 20. Landing flaps are 39.

## 4. Systems (as modelled)

Build order is in `createSystems.ts` → `createG800Systems(ctx, opts)`:
failures → logic → electrical → APU → fuel → hydraulics → pneumatics → pressurization → ice →
fire → oxygen → ADC ×3 → IRS ×3 → RA ×2 → gear → radios → FMS → Epic suite (Symmetry) →
thrust ratings → AFCS → A/T → FADEC → starts → stall warning → FBW → roll/yaw trim →
flaps → spoilers → steering → brakes → overspeed → altitude alert → TAWS → TCAS → TOCW →
CAS → post-logic → lights.

### 4.1 Electrical (`systems/electrical.ts`; SCQ electrical, GVI)

- **Sources.**
  - Two IDGs of 40 kVA each: 115/200 VAC, 3-phase, 400 Hz.
  - APU generator, 40 kVA. Comes on line at 99 % APU speed.
  - External AC (GPU). Available when `ac.g800.gpu_avail` = 1.
  - RAT generator, 30 kVA. Needs at least 160 KCAS.
  - Two 53 Ah NiCd main batteries.
  - UPS battery, 24 V 10.5 Ah lithium. Powers the FCCs and the BFCU.
  - Emergency batteries. They connect when EMER PWR is ARM and an ESS DC bus drops below 20 V.
- **AC buses.**
  - L and R MAIN AC, L and R ESS AC, EMER AC.
  - Source priority for each MAIN AC bus is own IDG, then APU, then GPU. The logic writes `ac.g800.l_ac_src` / `r_ac_src`.
  - With BUS TIE in AUTO, the AC tie closes when one side has no source.
  - The RAT feeds EMER AC and, through the ESS links, the ESS buses (`ac.g800.rat_mode`).
- **DC buses.**
  - L and R MAIN DC come from the switchable L and R MAIN TRUs.
  - L and R ESS DC come from the L and R ESS TRUs, backed by the batteries through contactors.
  - EMER DC comes from the EMER TRU.
  - The DC tie and diodes are modelled.
- **Loads.**
  - Each load has a circuit breaker (`elec.<load>_powered`).
  - Fuel boost pumps are on the ESS DC buses. This lets the engines keep running on the batteries after a dual IDG loss.
  - The APU ECU and APU fuel pump are on the battery buses.
  - GALLEY and CABIN R are shed in flight when only one generator is on line (EST shed rule).
- **Cold-and-dark check.** Batteries ON gives the ESS DC buses about 24 V. The APU start draws on them.

### 4.2 Fuel (`systems/fuel.ts`)

- **Tanks and pumps.**
  - Left and right wing tanks feed the L and R feed nodes.
  - One boost pump per side. Each is OFF / AUTO / ON. AUTO runs whenever the engine fuel command is on.
  - One AC alternate pump per side, OFF or ON, as backup.
  - A dedicated DC APU fuel pump feeds the APU from the left tank.
- **Crossflow.** The CROSSFLOW valve connects the feeds for balancing.
- **Consumers.**
  - Engines take fuel when the FADEC fuel command is on and the fire handle is in. Suction feed works up to 25,000 ft.
  - The APU takes fuel while it is starting or running and its fire handle is in.
- **Heated fuel return.** HFR in AUTO or ON returns warm fuel to the tank to keep it above −37 °C. The logic writes `ac.g800.hfr_active`.
- **Imbalance.** `fuel.imbalance` at 1,000 lb drives the "Fuel Imbalance" caution.
- **SCOPE.** Hoppers, ejector scavenge and intertank transfer are not modelled separately.

### 4.3 Hydraulics (`systems/hydraulic.ts`; SCQ hydraulics)

- **Systems and pumps.**
  - Left and right systems at 3,000 psi.
  - One engine-driven pump per system. The fire handle shuts it off. There is no cockpit EDP switch; the suite hides the EDP keys.
- **AUX pump.** Electric, on AC, feeds the left system. OFF / ARM / ON. In ARM it runs when the left system is below 1,800 psi and there is no PTU source. It stops above about 2,700 psi (latched hysteresis). Its overheat gives "Aux Hydraulic Pump Overload".
- **PTU.** Transfers power from the right system to the left. OFF / ARM / ON. In ARM it runs when the left system is below 1,800 psi and the right is healthy, with hysteresis. "PTU Hydraulic Fail" shows when the PTU runs and left pressure stays below 1,500 psi.
- **Consumers.**
  - The left system drives the gear, nosewheel steering and brakes (EST).
  - Both systems drive the flight-control actuators and spoilers.
  - The EBHAs (electric backup) keep the FBW surfaces moving after a dual loss.
- **Accumulators.** 1,200 psi precharge. Reservoirs 3.0 gal (left) and 1.6 gal (right).

### 4.4 Bleed, ECS, pressurization (`systems/environment.ts`)

- **Bleed sources.** L and R engine bleed. APU bleed, which has a load-control valve. The isolation valve has AUTO / OPEN / CLOSED; AUTO opens it for engine starts and single-source operation.
- **Packs and zones.** Two packs, and ram air (guarded). Four temperature zones: cockpit, forward cabin, mid cabin, aft cabin (FSB: 4 zones on the G800).
- **Starts.** START MASTER shuts the packs and opens the isolation valve during starts. The ATS takes bleed air.
- **Pressurization (`PRESS_G800` preset).**
  - 68 m³ volume, 10.69 psid max, schedule 2,840 ft cabin at FL410.
  - Modes AUTO / SEMI (landing elevation) / MANUAL (rate command).
  - DUMP and RAM AIR dump the cabin.
  - "Cabin Pressure Low" at 8,000 ft cabin. Passenger masks drop at 14,000 ft.

### 4.5 Ice and rain (`systems/environment.ts`, logic)

- **Wing and cowl anti-ice.** Split WAI (L/R) and split CAI (L/R), each OFF / AUTO / ON (FSB App. 4). AUTO follows the ice detector and is inhibited on the ground (WAI) and above FL350 (WAI and CAI).
- **Probe heat.** Pitot 1–3 and static 1–2. AUTO or ON; AUTO is on whenever an engine runs.
- **Window heat.** L and R windshield, cabin windows and the EVS window.
- **Ice detection.** `ice.detected` drives the "Ice Detected" advisory.

### 4.6 APU (`createApu`, RE220 preset)

- APU MASTER, then START (momentary).
- Available at 99 %. Then APU GEN and APU BLEED can be used.
- Fuel from the APU pump. Its own fire loop and fire handle.

### 4.7 Fire protection (`createFire`)

- **Detection.** Dual-loop detection on each engine, single on the APU, and a smoke detector in the aft baggage compartment.
- **Handles and bottles.** Two engine bottles (left and right) and an APU handle.
  - An engine handle unlocks when there is a fire warning (`ac.g800.fire_l_unlock`). Pulling it closes that engine's fuel, bleed and hydraulic shut-offs.
  - Rotating the handle to SHOT 1 fires the right bottle. SHOT 2 fires the left bottle.
  - The APU handle rotated fires the left bottle (GVI).
- **Test.** The FIRE TEST button tests all loops and lights.

### 4.8 Oxygen (`createOxygen`)

- **Crew.** Crew bottle with supply ON/OFF and quick-donning masks with a regulator (NORMAL / 100 % / EMERGENCY).
- **Passengers.** Passenger supply OFF / AUTO / ON. AUTO deploys the masks at 14,000 ft cabin.

### 4.9 Landing gear, brakes, steering

- **Gear.**
  - The handle has a ground lock solenoid; DN LOCK RELEASE overrides it.
  - Alternate extension is a nitrogen blowdown T-handle (one shot, VLO 175 KCAS).
  - Horn rules (GVI): below 500 ft AGL with both power levers at idle, or flaps above 22°, with the gear not down. Above 22° of flaps the horn cannot be silenced.
- **Brakes.** Carbon brakes with anti-skid (no protection below 15 kt). Brake overheat above 450 °C. Parking brake.
- **Autobrake.** RTO / OFF / LOW / MED / HIGH. RTO arms on the ground and applies maximum braking if both levers come to idle above the arming speed. Holding the toe brakes on the runway while setting thrust does not disarm an armed RTO (EST, `pedalDisarmsArmedRto: false`); pedals still disarm an active autobrake.
- **Steering.**
  - Steer-by-wire nosewheel steering, controlled by the NWS switch.
  - Pedal authority is ±7° (FSB App. 4).
  - The tiller is on the left console only (FSB). Tiller authority is ±80° (EST).

### 4.10 Flight controls (FBW; `FlyByWire` block)

- **Architecture.** Full-authority fly-by-wire with BAE active control sidesticks. The two sticks are electronically cross-coupled and back-driven by the autopilot (BJT500, SC 2024-01741).
- **NORMAL law.**
  - Pitch is a C*-type law with automatic speed-referenced trim.
  - The pitch trim switch on the stick changes the reference speed. The AP DISC / TRIM SYNC button re-syncs the reference to the current IAS when the AP is off.
  - The AoA limit is 0.9 × stall AoA. "AOA Limiting" advisory shows when the limit is active.
  - Roll is a rate command with bank protection.
  - Yaw has coordination (ny loop). ELDAC (engine-out yaw compensation) is a small feed-forward, −0.3 × thrust asymmetry, below 200 KIAS.
- **ALTERNATE law.** The FCC reverts when ADC or AHRS data is lost. Stall protection is then unavailable. The reversion latches in the air until FLT CTRL RESET. It never latches on the ground.
- **DIRECT law.** The warning is "FCC Direct Mode".
- **Trims.** Stabilizer trim is through the FBW. Roll trim uses the pedestal rocker. Rudder trim uses the pedestal knob and AUTO CENTER button.
- **Flaps and spoilers.** Flaps UP / 10 / 20 / 39, with a "Flap Fail" caution. Speed brake handle 0..1. Ground spoilers are armed by a switch. The speed brake retracts automatically when the levers are advanced in flight ("Speed Brake Auto Retract").
- **Takeoff configuration warning.** Flaps, speed brake, trim and parking brake at takeoff thrust.
- **SCOPE.** The FBW reads adc1 and ahrs1 only; there is no triplex voting.

### 4.11 Avionics: Honeywell Symmetry (Primus Epic), `src/avionics/honeywell-epic`

- **Hardware.**
  - Four 14-inch DU-1310-2 landscape display units: L PFD, L MFD, R MFD, R PFD.
  - Two ESIS-5000 touch standby flight displays (SFDs) flanking the GP-700 guidance panel.
  - Five touch-screen controllers (TSCs): outboard L, pedestal L, pedestal R, outboard R, and the jump seat (not modelled).
  - Two cursor control devices (CCDs).
  - Three overhead panel touch screens (OHPTS) with the ELEC, FUEL, ECS, HYD, ICE, LIGHTS and ENGINE pages.
- **Sensors.** ADC ×3, IRS ×3 (align mode 2), RA ×2, GPS (auto position), NAV/ILS/DME/ADF/marker radios, TAWS, TCAS.
- **Engine window.** EPR, N1, TGT, N2 and fuel flow. N1 target = `fadec.n1_limit_pct`.
- **Synoptics.** Bound to the systems vars (bleed psi, electrical, fuel, hydraulics).
- **NAV source.** Selected with `epic.s1.nav_src`, which couples `ap.nav_source`.

## 5. AFCS and autothrottle

- **AFCS (`Afcs`, `AFCS_PRIMUS_EPIC` preset).**
  - Guidance panel modes: HDG, NAV (LNAV/VOR/LOC), APR (LOC + GS / LPV), BC, LOW BANK, FLCH, VS, FPA, VNAV, ALT.
  - AP, YD, A/T and FD 1/2 keys. SPD MAN/FMS. PFD CMD transfer.
  - TO/GA buttons on the power levers.
  - AP disconnect on the sidestick. Minimum engage and disengage heights as §3.2.
  - Gains are tuned for the G800 inertia (pitch Kp 0.035). The yaw damper function is inside the FBW, so the AFCS yaw damper is off.
- **Autothrottle (`Autothrottle`, bizjet).**
  - Speed and thrust (N1 target) modes.
  - A/T DISC buttons on the levers (`at.disc`). The A/T drives the lever position `ac.g800.tla{i}`.
  - Retard at 50 ft RA.
- **Mode logic set for the G800** (`createSystems.ts`, added by the check-ride verification, §12):
  - NAV armed on the ground captures LNAV / LOC only once airborne (`nav.groundCapture: false`); the FD keeps TO on the roll.
  - VNAV in the climb engages VFLCH at the FMS climb speed (`vnavClimb`); VASEL / ALT never pass the selected altitude (`altvBoundBySel`).
  - FLCH speed gain 0.2 deg/kt (EST; default 0.35 pitched through the horizon after flap retraction at light weight).
  - GP-700 speed source FMS (MAN key off): `G800Logic` copies the FMS target speed / Mach into the selected speed, so FLCH
    and the A/T fly the FMS schedule in every mode (the suite's MAN key only changed the window and PFD colour).
- **SCOPE.** No autoland. The AP must be off by 80 ft on an ILS, as the approach test does.

## 6. CAS messages (`systems/cas.ts`)

Levels: warning (red, master warning), caution (amber, master caution), advisory (blue).
The tags say where each text comes from: [EPIC] shared suite text, [C450] G450-family text, [SCQ] GVI quiz, [EST] composed in Gulfstream style.

## 7. Normal procedures (`checklists.ts`; Gulfstream style, EST from C450/GVI)

1. **Before Starting Engines.** Parking brake SET. L/R BATT ON, 20 V minimum. APU START, then AVAILABLE. APU GEN on line. EMER PWR ARM. Fire detection TESTED. IRSs ALIGNED. FMS initialized with the flight plan loaded. APU BLEED ON. L/R BOOST AUTO. Power levers IDLE. BEACON ON.
2. **Engine Start (right engine first).** START MASTER ON. Bleed pressure 28 psi minimum. R ENG START press, R ENGINE RUN to RUN. SVO/IGN go out at about 42 % HP. Check oil and hydraulic pressure. Repeat for the left engine. START MASTER OFF. L/R GEN on line.
   - In the model a start takes about 33 s to stable idle, with a peak ITT of about 620 °C.
3. **Before Taxi.** APU GEN and APU as required. L/R ENG BLEED ON. L/R PACK ON. Flight controls CHECKED, FCS NORMAL. Flaps 10 or 20. NOSEWHEEL STEERING ON. GND SPLR ARMED. TAXI light ON.
4. **Before Takeoff.** TRS TO and V-speeds SET. Flaps 10 or 20. Stabilizer trim in the green band. Speed brake RETRACTED. AUTOBRAKE RTO. Anti-ice as required. STROBE and LANDING lights ON. CAS CHECKED. Parking brake RELEASED.
5. **After Takeoff / Climb.** Gear UP. Flaps UP. AUTOBRAKE OFF. TRS CLB. Pressurization CHECKED. LANDING lights OFF above 10,000 ft.
6. **Descent / Approach.** Landing elevation SET/CHECKED. Altimeters SET. Approach and minimums SET. AUTOBRAKE as required. GND SPLR ARMED.
7. **Before Landing.** Gear DOWN, three green. Flaps 39. Speed brake RETRACTED. LANDING lights ON.
8. **Shutdown.** Parking brake SET. L/R ENGINE RUN to STOP. BEACON and exterior lights OFF. APU OFF. EMER PWR OFF. L/R BATT OFF.

## 8. Abnormal procedures (as the systems respond; tested in `failures.test.ts`)

- **Generator failure (IDG).**
  - "L Generator Off" caution. The AC tie closes and the other IDG powers both MAIN AC buses. The galley is shed.
  - Crew: GEN switch OFF, then ON to reset. If it does not reset, start the APU and select APU GEN.
- **Dual generator failure, APU off.**
  - Both MAIN AC buses fail and the batteries carry the ESS buses ("Main Batt Discharge"). Boost pumps on ESS DC keep the engines fed.
  - Crew: RAT DEPLOY (at least 160 KCAS). The RAT powers EMER AC and the ESS buses. "RAT Deployed" advisory. Start the APU below 37,000 ft.
- **Left hydraulic loss.**
  - "L Hydraulic Pressure Low". With PTU in ARM, the PTU restores left pressure from the right system.
  - If both systems are lost, AUX in ARM powers the left system ("Aux Hydraulic On").
  - With AUX failed too, the FBW keeps flying on the EBHAs. Use alternate gear extension (blowdown) and expect no nosewheel steering.
- **Engine fire.**
  - "L Engine Fire" warning, aural, and the handle unlocks.
  - Crew: power lever IDLE, ENGINE RUN STOP, pull the fire handle. This closes fuel, bleed and EDP. Rotate to SHOT 1 (right bottle). If the warning persists after 30 s, SHOT 2 (left bottle).
  - The FBW yaw channel and ELDAC hold sideslip below 3° with the engine out.
- **ADC or IRS failure.** "FCC Alternate Mode" and "Stall Protection Unavail". The reversion latches. After the source is restored, FLT CTRL RESET (guarded) returns to NORMAL law. Limit speed 285 KCAS / M0.90.
- **Rapid decompression.**
  - "Cabin Pressure Low" warning above 8,000 ft cabin. Passenger masks deploy at 14,000 ft ("Passenger Oxygen On").
  - Crew: masks ON, 100 %, emergency descent.
- **Engine failure on takeoff.** V1 action. ELDAC and the FBW yaw law limit the heading excursion below 200 KIAS.
- **RTO.** Both levers to IDLE above the arming speed with AUTOBRAKE RTO gives maximum braking. Ground spoilers deploy when armed.
- **Takeoff configuration warning.** Advancing to takeoff thrust with the parking brake set, flaps not 10/20, speed brake out or trim out of the green band gives an aural warning and CAS (`alert.takeoff_config`).

## 9. Cockpit control inventory

Conventions:
- Every control is listed with its label, type, positions, spring or guard, backlight, var and approximate position and size.

## 10. Integration notes for the cockpit, exterior and index.ts agents

- **Creating the systems.** `createG800Systems(ctx, { canvas })` returns `G800Systems` with `suite` (the `EpicSuite`), `failures`, `fbw`, `gear`, `brakes` and the other blocks. Use `applyG800State(ctx, sys, state)` for the presets: `cold_dark`, `ready_to_taxi`, `takeoff`, `cruise`, `approach`.

- **Input.** `G800_INPUT_MAP` maps the throttles to `tla`, the reversers to `rev`, flap detents 0–3, gear 0/1, speed brake detents [0, 0.5, 1] and the parking brake. The AP toggle event is `ap.ap` and the A/T disconnect is `at.disc`.
- **Exterior animation vars.**
  - Surfaces: `surf.*`.
  - Gear: `gear.pos{0..2}`.
  - Flaps: `surf.flaps_deg`.
  - Reversers: `eng{i}.reverser_pos`.
  - Lights: `light.<name>` from `systems/lighting.ts` (nav, beacon, beacon_lower, strobe, strobe_tail, landing_l, landing_r, recognition, taxi, wing, logo, emer). Cockpit dimmers: `ac.light.panel`, `ac.light.flood`, `ac.light.dome`, `ac.light.seatbelt`, `ac.light.no_smoking`.
  - RAT: `ac.g800.rat_deployed`.
  - Main door: `ac.door.main`.
- **Sidestick.** Import from `src/cockpit/controls`. Pass `backDriveWhenVar: 'ap.engaged'` and back-drive vars for the AP servo commands, or leave them unset to show the pilot input only.

  - Fire-handle strip (main cockpit, `FIRE_STRIP`).
  - ELECTRIC POWER CONTROL: L GEN, APU GEN, GPU, R GEN, BUS TIE / L BATT, R BATT, EMER PWR
    (clear guard), APU MASTER, APU START. ENGINE START: START MASTER, CRANK MASTER, CONT IGN /

  - Three OHPTS (Epic `addOhpts`, pages ELEC / FUEL / ECS by default).
  - BLEED AIR: L ENG, APU, R ENG / RAM AIR (red guard), ISOLATION (CLOSED / AUTO / OPEN),
    L PACK, R PACK. CABIN PRESSURE CONTROL: MODE (AUTO / SEMI / MAN), LDG ELEV knob, MAN
    DESC / HOLD / CLIMB (spring), DUMP (red guard).
  - SYSTEM TEST (FIRE TEST, LAMP TEST), EMER LTS (OFF / ARM / ON, guarded in ARM), COCKPIT
    LIGHTS (PANEL and FLOOD dimmers, DOME, STORM), RAT manual T-handle.
  - Source for the hardware set: FlightGlobal 2018 G500 analysis, via a search-engine excerpt only
    ("only four traditional panels: engine start, electrical power control, bleed air and cabin
    pressure control"). Legends follow the GV-family panels of the same names. The layout is EST.
  - Every hardware switch writes the same `GULFSTREAM_OVERHEAD_VARS` var as the matching touch key.
    Switchlight legends read system state. Examples: GEN amber OFF = generator not on line; GPU

    BATT DISCH (battery current below −8 A). They are powered from the ESS DC buses and respond

  - `ac.g800.storm_lt` (STORM switch) → `systems/lighting.ts` 'storm' dimmer → `ac.light.storm`.
    That drives one white storm flood (EST 12 cd) in the overhead console. The `panel_lts` load
    draws 2 A more with it on.
  - `states.ts` resets the storm light in every preset.
- **Side consoles.**
  - Left: TILLER (spring-centred wheel, `ac.g800.tiller`, ±80° nosewheel), aft of the sidestick pod.
  - Each side: crew O2 mask stowage box (`ac.g800.oxy_mask{1,2}`), regulator NORM / 100% / EMER
    (`ac.g800.oxy_mode{1,2}`) and a green O2 FLOW indicator (`oxy.pilot_flow_lpm` /
    `oxy.copilot_flow_lpm`).
  - In NORM (diluter demand) at a low cabin altitude the model gives no flow; 100 % / EMER flow.
- **CB panels.**
  - On the sidewalls aft of each seat (x 11.76, z −0.2; EST, GVI-family location).
  - Every breaker of the electrical network (87) is a pullable `CircuitBreaker` on `cb.<load>` /
    `cb.<load>_tripped`, grouped by bus. A test checks that the table equals `breakerNames()`.
  - Pulling one removes its load's power. Examples: DU 1 blanks, NAV lights go out, FCC + BFCU
    gives `fcc.power_ok` 0.
  - An `elec.<load>.short` failure trips it, and it re-trips while the fault persists.
  - SCOPE: BJT500 says 45 % of the G500's breakers are electronic (ECBs, reset from a touch page).
    Here all of them are mechanical.

  - Windshield wipers: EST none; the GVI/GVIII family has heated, coated windshields.
  - Hardware audio panels: Symmetry audio is on the TSC RADIOS app.
  - Flight-deck door lock control: no public G800 data and no door system to drive.
  - Map / chart lights: the real-light budget is ≤ 5; 4 are used.

## 11. Scope, simplifications and open issues

- **No public G800 AFM.** System ratings (IDG/RAT kVA, battery, hydraulic logic, horn rules, VFE 10/20, VLE, VA, APU limits) are GVI values. The FSB lists no G800 difference for them.
- **Estimated values.** V-speeds and stall speeds come from the FDM CLmax design values. Cruise fuel flow comes from Breguet on the GAC range numbers.
- **CAS texts.** Some texts are Gulfstream-style estimates. They are tagged [EST] in the code.
- **Engine display.** EPR is derived from N1.
- **Not modelled.** Hoppers and intertank transfer, PTT, the fifth (jump-seat) TSC, autoland, force-feel on the sidesticks, and triplex FCC voting (the FBW reads adc1 and ahrs1).
- **A/T hunting.** The A/T moves the levers by about ±0.1 in cruise. Speed holds within the test tolerance, but `speedKp` could be tuned.
- **Flaps 39 placard mismatch.** `G800_AIRFRAME.flapPlacardKt` in the shared avionics config shows 180 kt for flaps 39. The FSB value is 190 KCAS. That file is outside this module and was not changed.
- **Not built here.** Cockpit geometry, exterior and `index.ts` belong to other agents.
- **Found by the check ride, not fixed (shared code):**
  - Spawning in the app at KSAV RWY 10 (`ready_to_taxi`) the aircraft started ~4 ft above the terrain and "crashed"
    (hard landing 1,259 fpm) in the headless screenshot run. The Longitude also spawned airborne there (it dropped ~3 ft
    without exceeding its limit); KTEB is fine. Suspected cause: the world's `ensureLoaded` timeout (25 s) resolving on
    the fallback airport elevation under load, then the z14 terrain arriving lower. World/app code, not changed.
  - The TRS stays in CLB at cruise; the crew must select CRZ on the TSC (no automatic CRZ at level-off).
  - PERF INIT speed defaults come from the suite (climb 250/.80, cruise 300/.85, descent 280/.80), not from the G800
    FMS configuration; the crew enters the cruise speed.
  - The FMS distance to destination includes the missed-approach legs.
  - The EGPWS terrain layer paints the airport surroundings solid yellow on the ground (no runway-elevation band suppression).
  - GP-700 key legends are hard to read in daylight screenshots (suite cockpit helper).

## 12. Check-ride verification (`tests/aircraft/g800/verify/fullFlight.test.ts`)

One continuous headless flight, KSAV → KATL, cold & dark to cold & dark, flown only through cockpit
control vars, GP-700 events, the pedestal TSC FMS app (MCDU key presses) and the stick / pedals /
tiller / toe brakes. About 47 min of sim time, ~85 s wall time.

Defects found and fixed by this check ride:
- LNAV went active on the runway (armed NAV captured on the ground). Now captures after lift-off.
- The VNAV key in the climb only armed VPATH. Now VFLCH (Primus Epic VNAV climb).
- FLCH after flap retraction at 70,000 lb pitched to −4° and −2,300 fpm at 2,700 ft AGL. FLCH gain reduced.
- GP-700 FMS speed had no effect on the A/T or FLCH. Now the FMS target drives the selected speed.
- Holding the brakes for the static thrust set disarmed the RTO autobrake. Additive option in the shared `Brakes`.
- PERF INIT showed the suite's G650 tail number; set to N800GA.
