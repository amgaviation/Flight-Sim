# Gulfstream G650 (GVI) — research dossier and implementation reference

Ultra-long-range business jet: 2 × Rolls-Royce BR700-725A1-12, three-axis fly-by-wire with conventional
yokes, Honeywell Primus Epic "PlaneView II" flight deck. This document collects the public data behind
`src/aircraft/g650/**` and is the contract for the cockpit build (`src/aircraft/g650/cockpit`, another agent):
every control listed in §9 has a var in `vars.ts` that a system in `systems/**` consumes.

Model (`fdm.ts br725`): corrected N1 96 % gives MTO thrust (EST, 98.5 % physical at 30 °C < 102.8 limit);
lapse `δ^0.92 (1 − 0.5M + 0.4M²)/√θ` EST; TSFC 0.0375 kg/N/h at rated SL static (0.368 lb/lbf/h, EST) with a
Mach factor 0.93 calibrated to AIN; ground idle EST 24 % LP / 62 % HP / TGT ~440 °C / 480 lb/h per engine
(BR700-family class); start peak TGT ~560 °C; self-sustaining at 36 % HP so the engine accelerates after
the 42 % starter cut-out.

Thrust ratings (`systems/engines.ts`): TO, GA, CLB, CRZ, MCT tables in corrected N1 (EST shapes; MCT =
66.6/75.2 of MTO at sea level), flat rated to ISA+15, clamped to 102.8 % LP. The thrust levers use a detent
law: CRZ 0.6, CLB 0.8, MAX (TO/GA) 1.0 of the forward travel (EST positions); integral reverse range −1..0.

## 4. Systems

### 4.1 Electrical (LUC, SCQ, LIM) — `systems/electrical.ts`, logic in `systems/logic.ts`

- AC: L IDG → L MAIN AC, R IDG → R MAIN AC (40 kVA 115 V 400 Hz each); APU GEN (40 kVA, SL-45,000 ft) and EXT AC
  (40 kVA cart) on the AC tie bus; L / R BUS TIE relays (AUTO / ISLN) connect a main AC bus to the tie bus when its
  IDG is off line: priority on-side IDG > APU > EXT > cross-side IDG. Sources are never paralleled.
- EMER AC fed from L MAIN AC; RAT GEN (15 kVA LUC; 30 kVA SCQ) after the twist-and-pull deployment, on line
  ≥ 180 KCAS, feeds EMER AC and (through the ESS AC buses) the L / R ESS TRUs (LIM).
- Five 250 A TRUs: L ESS, L MAIN, AUX, R MAIN, R ESS; L/R MAIN TRU switches select the opposite main AC ("R AC"
  / "L AC"); the AUX TRU substitutes for a failed TRU (priority ESS before MAIN, L before R) and sheds AUX DC
  (cabin). Ground TRU loads limited to 80 % (AUX 40 %) (LIM).
- Two 28 V 53 Ah NiCd main batteries on the L/R battery buses, switched to the ESS DC buses (16 min of ESS DC);
  the APU starts from the left battery (≥ 22 V). Emergency power: two 24 V 10.5 Ah sealed lead-acid E-BATTs for the
  L/R EMERGENCY and FLIGHT INSTRUMENT buses (SMCs, 3 IRUs, ACPs, MCDU 1/3, NAV/COM 1), 45 min; ON, or ARM and
  either ESS DC < 20 V. Flight-control batteries: EBHA (NiCd 28 V 53 Ah → 7 MCEs) and UPS (lead acid 24 V 10.5 Ah
  → FCC 1A, 2B, BFCU), 30 min.
- Load shedding: galley and 60 Hz cabin converters shed in flight on a single AC source (LUC: inhibited on the ground).
- SCOPE: one `emer_dc` bus for the L/R EMERGENCY + FLIGHT INSTRUMENT buses; chargers modelled by the batteries
  floating on their buses; the static inverter (CPC ch 1) is not a separate component; GSB fed from R MAIN DC or
  the R battery.

### 4.2 Fuel (LUC, LIM, TCDS) — `systems/fuel.ts`

Two integral wing tanks with root hoppers (kept full by flapper valves and ejectors); two 25 psi brushless boost
pumps per hopper: MAIN (L/R ESS DC) and ALT (L/R MAIN DC); "all operable boost pumps ON in all phases" (LIM).
Suction feed below ~20,000 ft. Valves: engine SOVs (fire handles), crossflow (L ESS DC), intertank (gravity
between hoppers, R ESS DC). APU from the left manifold. Low fuel 650 lb per side; imbalance limits 2,000 lb in
flight, 1,000 lb for takeoff; tank temperature −37 / +54 °C; heated fuel return (AUTO on at 0 °C tank, off at 10 °C,
inhibited with crossflow open, fire handle pulled, < 600 lb). SCOPE: the hopper is not a separate tank. While the HFRS runs
it warms its tank (per-tank skin-temperature bias in `systems/fuel.ts`, EST +55 °C on the effective skin:
at TAT −45 °C the tank equilibrates near +10 °C, reproducing the 0 → +10 °C AUTO hysteresis) and posts the
"Fuel Return On" advisory.

### 4.3 Hydraulics (LUC, LIM) — `systems/hydraulic.ts`

Two 3,000 psi systems. LEFT: EDP (25-37 gpm), gear, inboard brakes, flaps, main door, midboard spoilers, NWS,
L reverser, flight controls. RIGHT: EDP, outboard brakes, inboard/outboard spoilers, R reverser, flight
controls, PTU motor. AUX pump (DC, L ESS, 2 gpm into the LEFT system): OFF/ARM/ON — ARM latches on the ground with
low L pressure and a brake pedal, in flight for gear/flaps after an EDP loss (>100 KCAS); ON manual, 2 min timer in
flight. PTU (PWR XFR UNIT, 22 gpm, R drives a pump in L): ARM = auto when L < 2,400 psi for 7 s, off 7 s after
L ≥ 2,750. Seven EBHAs (self-contained) are the backup actuators for the flight controls. Fire handles close the
hydraulic shutoff valves. After a hydraulic failure: 285 KCAS / M0.90; LIM flight-time limits.

### 4.4 Flight controls (LUC, SCQ, AIN, LIM) — `createSystems.ts` (FlyByWire), `systems/logic.ts`

- Two dual-channel FCCs + BFCU, 16 REUs, 9 HA + 7 EBHA actuators; yokes mechanically interconnected; speed-stable
  (trim sets a reference speed in the air); on the ground pitch trim moves the stabilizer (0.4°/s; backup 0.15°/s).
- Laws: NORMAL (sub-modes on ground / takeoff-landing / cruise; AOA limiting 0.87-0.93 normalized AOA, max 0.96 at
  full aft yoke, "won't fully stall"; high-speed protection above Vmo/Mmo + 5; bank return to 33°);
  ALTERNATE ("FCC Alternate Mode", fewer than two valid ADS / invalid IRS; FLT CTRL RESET may return to NORMAL;
  no AP, no AOA protection); DIRECT ("FCC Direct Mode"); BACKUP (BFCU): with every FCC channel dead but the
  UPS-powered BFCU alive the shared FlyByWire runs the fourth mode (mode_code 3, `backupPower`,
  additive): direct-law gains, no yaw damper, no ground spoilers, CAS "FCC Backup Mode" (EST text).
- Spoilers: 6 panels; speed brake 30° in flight, auto-retract at 95 % TRA or high AOA ("Speed Brake Auto
  Retract"); roll spoilers; ground spoilers (GND SPOILER ARMED, levers idle, wheel speed > 47 kt or both WOW +
  RA < 10 ft); no ground spoilers in DIRECT/BACKUP; "Aircraft Configuration" red with speed brake and flaps 39 / gear
  down in flight.
- Flaps UP / 10 / TO-20 / DOWN 39 (FECU, L hydraulics / AUX / PTU).
- Pedestal: BACKUP PITCH (split, guarded), FLT CTRL RESET, ROLL MOTOR CONTROL, AUTO CENTER, GND SPOILER,
  GPWS/GND SPLR FLAP ORIDE, aileron and rudder trim.
- Model: shared `FlyByWire` (nz-command law, EST gains kp 0.3 / ki 0.15 / kq 0.03 at 250 kt); AOA limiter target
  0.90 of the stall AOA with onset 0.96 × that (tuned so the demonstrated maximum stays ≈ 0.96-0.97 normalized, test);
  an ALTERNATE latch with FLT CTRL RESET in `G650Logic`; BACKUP PITCH drives the FBW stabilizer directly
  (`G650PostLogic`). SCOPE / known limit: in a zoom climb at idle with full aft yoke the nz-based law can still
  depart into a stall recovery (the attitude-limit protection overrides the AOA limiter in the nose-down recovery);
  normal decelerations are protected.

### 4.5 Landing gear, brakes, steering (LUC, LIM)

LGCU (2 lanes: L ESS DC, R MAIN DC), L hydraulics (PTU / AUX backup); EMER LDG GEAR T-handle: N2 blowdown, ~6 s,
one shot (≤ 175 KCAS). Horn: below 500 ft, both levers idle, flaps < 22° (HORN SILENCE); flaps > 22° any gear not
down (cannot be silenced). Steer-by-wire (NWSCU L ESS DC, L hyd, NWS POWER guarded switch): tiller ±80°, pedals ±7°.
Brake-by-wire (BCU ch A L ESS / ch B R ESS): inboard L hyd, outboard R hyd; separate inboard (L system) and
outboard (R system) accumulators (700 psi N2) with their own INBD / OUTBD gauge scales (`V.accumInbdPsi/OutbdPsi`,
G650Logic); with both BCU channels dead there is NO pedal braking ("Brake by Wire Fail") — the accumulators then
serve only the emergency/park handle, which meters braking in PROPORTION to its travel and sets the parking
brake near full travel (~0.9); anti-skid to 10 kt; autobrake RTO / OFF / LOW (7 ft/s²) / MED (10) / HIGH (max anti-skid; EST
16 ft/s²); "Brake Overheat" > 600 °C.

### 4.6 APU — Honeywell RE220 (LUC, LIM)

APU CONTROL: MASTER (READY in 10-16 s), START, STOP, FIRE EXT (LEFT bottle), EGT/RPM readouts. Starter from the
left main battery (≥ 22 V; external DC start prohibited); generator on line at 99 % + 2 s; bleed after 60 s;
264 lb/h; EGT 1,050 °C start / 732 °C running; 106 %; 45,000 ft; starter-assisted engine starts < 30,000 ft;
in-flight starts inhibited above 37,000 ft (§7); cooldown 60 s. APU bleed cannot pressurize in flight (modelled: LCV only on the ground).

### 4.7 Pneumatics and air conditioning (LUC)

5th/8th-stage bleed per engine, PRSOV to 40 psi (ESS DC + pressure), ISOLATION valve (AUTO opens with START/CRANK
MASTER or APU bleed on the ground; OPEN; CLOSED). Two packs (tail), zones cockpit (R ACC), forward and aft
cabin (L ACC), trim air; RAM AIR (packs off, "Ram Air Selected On"). START/CRANK MASTER shuts the R pack, a start in
progress the L pack. Single pack max altitude 48,000 ft.

### 4.8 Pressurization (LUC, LIM, GAC, AIN)

CPC (2 channels) + thrust-recovery outflow valve; max 10.69 psi; relief 10.80 / 11.00 psi; negative relief
−0.25 psi; 0.3 psi max for taxi/takeoff/landing; cabin 3,290 ft at FL410 (AIN) and 4,850 ft at FL510 (GAC);
pre-pressurisation 0.25 psi; climb 500 fpm, descent 150-300 fpm to LFE − 250 ft; AUTO (FMS landing field) / SEMI
(crew entry) / MANUAL (MAN HOLD knob); "Cabin Pressure Low" trip altitude by landing field elevation (8,000 ft up
to LFE 7,500 ft; 10,000 to 9,500; 14,500 to 14,000; 15,500 above); passenger masks 14,750 ft (15,750 HIGH ALT).
SCOPE: FLIGHT/LANDING only selects whether the CPC profiles the climb to the FMS cruise altitude. Emergency
Descent Mode: cabin altitude above the warning trip for 5 s in flight latches `ac.g650.press.edm` and the blue
"Emergency Descent Mode" advisory (EST); the AFCS-coupled automatic descent itself is not modelled (SCOPE).

### 4.9 Ice and rain (LUC, LIM)

Four rotary knobs L WING, L COWL, R COWL, R WING (OFF / AUTO / ON); AUTO from two ice detectors (L/R MAIN AC),
inhibited above 35,000 ft; cowl valves close 3 min and wing valves 5 min after the last ice. WAI 130 °F target
("Wing Temperature Low" after 2 min). Four heated air-data probes (ANTI-ICE HTR 1-4, heated after engine start),
windshield L/R, cabin windows, EVS window. No wipers (hydrophobic coating). SCOPE: a single airframe-ice value
protected as well as the weaker wing.

### 4.10 Fire (LUC, LIM)

Dual-loop engine detectors (FDCU L/R), L/R fire handles (pull: fuel SOV, hydraulic SOV, bleed, IDG; rotate outward
= shot 1 RIGHT bottle, inward = shot 2 LEFT bottle, either bottle to either engine); APU helium-tube detector, FIRE
EXT discharges the LEFT bottle; ENGINE FIRE TEST (L/R LOOP A/B, FAULT TEST) and APU TEST — each test switch exercises only its own zone
(per-zone test overrides in `createFire`): L LOOP A/B light the L engine warning alone, APU TEST the APU alone.
Pulling a handle also trips that side's IDG (electrical.ts) and is treated as a commanded shutdown (no red
"Engine Fail").

### 4.11 Oxygen (LUC)

Two bottles (crew / passenger gauges), EROS quick-donning masks (N / 100 % / EMERGENCY), PASS OXYGEN shutoff,
PASSENGER OXYGEN OFF / AUTO / MAN, "Passenger Oxygen On".

### 4.12 Avionics (EPIC, LUC, LIM)

Primus Epic PlaneView II: 4 × 14-in DUs, 2 SMCs (standby + display controller), guidance panel, 3 MCDUs, 2 CCDs;
3 ADS (MFP) + standby ADS 4; 3 hybrid IRUs (GPS updating, align on power-up; LIM: aligns to 78° latitude);
2 RAs; IRS MODE SELECT panel (IRS 1 / 2 / 3 switchlights on the lower centre panel, ON / amber ON BAT, SmartCockpit G650 avionics quiz); IRUs align only up to 78° latitude (LIM; Irs `maxAlignLatDeg`); EGPWS class A with
SmartRunway/RAAS ("Approaching runway XX" / "On runway XX" [+ "<n> hundred remaining" EST below 6,000 ft],
`systems/raas.ts`, gated by the pedestal RAAS INHIBIT), TCAS II; autopilot min engage 200 ft AGL, min disengage 80 ft on ILS/LPV (LIM); A/T: TO
HOLD to 400 ft, RETARD below 50 ft (LUC). HUD/EVS page state only (suite).

## 6. Normal procedures (abridged, `checklists.ts`)

0. **Cockpit Preparation** (abridged preflight): gear handle DOWN, fire handles IN, FUEL CONTROL OFF, power
   levers IDLE, speed brake RETRACT, parking brake SET, fuel quantity.
1. **Before Starting Engines** (code450 order): MAIN BATTERIES ON (22 V minimum); FLT CTRL BATTERIES EBHA, UPS ON
   (SPOST); EMERGENCY POWER ARM; EMER LTS ARM; IRS ON; fire detection FAULT TEST and L/R ENG fire tests TESTED;
   fuel MAIN and ALT pumps ON **before** the APU (a no-boost RE220 start hangs with no light-off); APU per the
   APU Start checklist → generator on line; BUS TIES AUTO; MAIN TRUs NORM; CABIN / GALLEY MASTER as required;
   X-FLOW / INTER TANK closed; AUX PUMP / PWR XFR UNIT ARM; engine BLEED AIR OFF for the start; ISOLATION AUTO;
   anti-ice OFF; window heat ON; oxygen ON / AUTO; CABIN PRESSURE AUTO; SEAT BELT / NO SMOKE ON; gear 3 green;
   power levers IDLE; speed brake RETRACT; aileron/rudder trim zero; NWS POWER OFF (ON after start); altimeters
   (3) SET and cross-checked; V-speeds ENTERED; guidance panel SET; parking brake SET; FUEL CONTROL OFF; BEACON
   ON; takeoff briefing.
1a. **APU Start** (code450): EMERGENCY POWER ON; batteries 22 V; APU fire test; L MAIN boost pump ON; MASTER ON;
   READY (10-16 s); START; APU GEN on line; EMERGENCY POWER back to ARM. A failed start is retried after a
   MASTER cycle within the starter duty limits (LIM 3 min / 15 s × 2; the master cycle clears the ECU fault).
2. **Starting Engines**: APU BLEED ON (after 60 s); START MASTER ON (isolation opens, packs off); bleed ≥ 40 psi;
   R ENG start, FUEL CONTROL R RUN; monitor light-off, TGT < 700 °C, idle; **PTU pressure CHECK 3,000
   (+300/−400) psi** (the R engine is started first to demonstrate the PTU carrying the left system); L engine
   the same; START MASTER OFF.
   Alternate: CRANK MASTER + L/R ENG (latched) for dry motoring; with FUEL CONTROL RUN and CONT IGN a manual start.
3. **After Starting Engines**: GENs on line, BLEEDs ON, APU as required, hydraulics 3,000 psi, probe heaters ON,
   NWS POWER ON.
4. **Taxi / Line Up** (published titles): brakes CHECK on first movement, flight controls CHECK (FCC Normal),
   thrust reversers first flight of day, flaps 20 (or 10), trim in the green band, GND SPOILER ARMED, AUTOBRAKE
   RTO, TRS TO, IRS 1-2-3 aligned, lights; parking brake released, speed brake stowed, transponder TA/RA;
   anti-ice ON 2 min before takeoff thrust when required (LIM).
5. **Takeoff**: MAX (A/T TO), rotate at VR to ~12°, gear up with positive rate, flaps up by V2 + 30 (EST); A/T HOLD
   to 400 ft then climb; AP ≥ 200 ft AGL.
6. **Climb / cruise**: FLCH 250 kt below 10,000 ft, 300 KIAS / M0.85 (EST schedule), cruise M0.85 (LRC) to M0.90;
   altimeters STD at transition, landing lights off at 10,000 ft; cruise scan of pressurization and fuel balance.
7. **Approach / landing**: landing field and minimums set; APR (LOC/GS) with NAV SRC LOC; gear down and flaps 39 at
   glideslope capture; VREF + 5; AP off by 80 ft (ILS) / 200 ft; RETARD at 50 ft; ground spoilers; reverse to idle
   by 60 KCAS; autobrake as selected.
7a. **Descent / After Landing**: altimeters SET and cross-checked, seat belts ON, fuel balance; after landing
   flaps UP, transponder STANDBY, radar OFF, anti-ice as required, APU for the arrival.
8. **Shutdown** (code450, ~29 published items abridged to the modelled controls): parking brake SET; transponder
   STANDBY; PTU and AUX PUMP NOT ARM; SEAT BELT / NO SMOKE OFF; 3 min idle (LUC), FUEL CONTROL OFF; OXYGEN
   systems OFF; NWS POWER OFF; IRS OFF; CABIN / GALLEY MASTERS OFF; EMERGENCY POWER OFF; BLEED AIR OFF; APU
   STOP; boost pumps OFF; anti-ice / probe heaters OFF; exterior lights OFF; window heat OFF; FLT CTRL
   BATTERIES OFF (left on they discharge into the FCS buses); MAIN BATTERIES OFF; chocks.
9. **Securing**: EMER LTS OFF, ground service bus as required, interior lights OFF, door, covers / pins.

## 7. Abnormal procedures (abridged)

- **Engine fire**: thrust lever IDLE, FUEL CONTROL OFF, fire handle PULL, rotate outward (shot 1, RIGHT bottle); if
  the warning persists after 30 s rotate inward (shot 2, LEFT bottle).
- **APU fire**: APU MASTER OFF, FIRE EXT (LEFT bottle).
- **Generator fail**: GEN OFF → ON (one reset / AC/DC RESET); bus ties AUTO; APU if needed (in flight ≤ 37,000 ft).
- **Dual generator loss**: batteries hold the ESS DC buses (16 min); APU start; RAT deploy (≥ 180 KCAS; +6 % fuel).
- **Hydraulic system fail**: PWR XFR UNIT ARM/ON, AUX PUMP ARM; 285 KCAS / M0.90; flight-time limits (LIM).
- **Cabin Pressure Low**: crew masks 100 %, passenger oxygen MAN, emergency descent.
- **FCC Alternate Mode**: FLT CTRL RESET once; if not recovered: 285 KCAS / M0.90, no icing, landing crosswind
  10 kt, VREF + 10 (LIM).
- CAS-selectable lists in `checklists.ts` (Epic ECL opens the checklist from the message): L/R Engine Fire (with
  the confirm / 10 s pacing and the core-fire "do NOT discharge" note), APU Fire, L/R Autostart Abort, L/R
  Engine Fail (air start below 30,000 ft), L/R and L-R Generator Fail (APU restart at/below FL370 only), L/R Hyd
  System Fail, FCC Alternate / Direct Mode, Cabin Pressure Low (masks ON at **100 %**), Emergency Descent, Fuel
  Level Low, Fuel Imbalance.

## 11. Known simplifications and open points

- Gear stations, empty CG, engine position and panel geometry are EST (no public station data).
- EPR is derived for display (Epic `eprFromN1Br700`, EST); the engine model is N1-controlled.
- AOA protection uses the shared nz-command FBW law with EST gains; see §4.4 for the zoom-climb limitation.
- HUD/EVS imagery, alternate-start ignition logic subtleties, the refuel panel and the 60 Hz cabin converters are
  simplified or not modelled. Deliberate electrical SCOPEs (single `emer_dc` bus, single-phase CBs, folded static
  inverter, one 60 Hz load) are documented in `systems/electrical.ts` and kept.
- EPR is a display/mode state only (`ac.g650.eng{i}_epr_mode`, "Engine Alternate Mode" CAS on an EPR-sensing
  failure); the physics stays N1-controlled.
- The cockpit (`cockpit/`), exterior and `index.ts` are built by other agents from this document.

## 14. Check-ride verification (`tests/aircraft/g650/verify/`)

`fullFlight.test.ts` flies one continuous KSAV -> KATL flight headless (real FDM, full systems, Epic suite on
fake canvases, real navigation database, two-field world), acting only through cockpit control vars,
guidance-panel keys, MCDU 1 keys and the yoke / pedals / toe brakes / tiller:

cold & dark -> batteries, FLT CTRL BATTERIES, EMERGENCY POWER / EMER LTS ARM -> APU battery start (on line
~34 s, battery bus dip ~22.7 V) -> APU bleed, START MASTER, R then L auto start (~27 s each, peak TGT
~560 °C < 700) -> GENs / BLEEDs, APU off -> IRS alignment (~4 min) -> MCDU: KSAV KATL, RW10, MCN, ILS 26R via
HAINZ, PERF INIT FL300 / BOW / pax, ACTIVATE -> tiller taxi and line-up -> A/T + TO/GA (FMA TO / TO, LNAV armed,
A/T TO -> HOLD -> THR), autobrake RTO -> lift-off 128 KIAS at ~1,820 ft (71,500 lb, VR 111) -> AP, LNAV, FLCH,
flaps up, VNAV (VFLCH 250 kt below 10,000 ft, 300 KIAS above) -> FL300 in ~7.7 min -> cruise M0.85, ~501 KTAS,
~4,470 lb/h, tanks decrement at the engine flow -> VNAV descent (VPATH, FMS SPD) -> APR (NAV SRC to LOC1,
auto-tuned ILS), LOC / GS, gear, flaps 20 / 39 within VFE / VLO, VREF + 5 -> AP off at 80 ft (LIM ILS
minimum), hand flare, RETARD -> ground spoilers, reversers (max N1 < 78.1 %), autobrake MED -> taxi clear ->
3 min cool-down, FUEL CONTROL OFF (no amber CAS) -> cold & dark.

| Defect | Fix |
