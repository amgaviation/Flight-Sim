# Cessna 172S Skyhawk SP: research dossier and shared core

This is the shared dossier for both 172S cockpit variants:

- **c172-steam**: analog six-pack, Bendix/King stack (KMA 26, KX 155A ×2, KR 87, KT 76C, GPS), KAP 140. Source is POH 172SPHUS Rev 5, serials 172S8704 and on.
- **c172-g1000**: Garmin G1000 NXi (GDU 1050 PFD/MFD, GMA 1347, GIA 63W ×2, GEA 71, GDC 74A, GRS 79, GTX 345) with the GFC 700 AFCS. Source is POH 172SPHAUS-03 NAV III plus the Garmin NXi Pilot's Guide 190-02177-00.

The airframe, engine, fuel system, flight controls, limitations and Section 5 performance are the same in both POHs. I compared them page by page. The two variants differ in electrical architecture, vacuum (two pumps vs one), instruments and lighting.

The shared implementation lives in `src/aircraft/c172s-common/`. Headless tests are in `tests/aircraft/c172s-common/`. Section 10 gives the integration recipe for the variant modules.

Conventions used in this file:

- "POH" means 172SPHUS Rev 5.
- "POH NAV III" means 172SPHAUS-03.
- "EST" marks a value estimated with the reasoning given.
- "SCOPE" marks a simplification.

## 6. Systems

### 6.1 Electrical: common architecture

The following are from POH Sec 7, POH NAV III Sec 7 and UND. Model: `systems/electrical.ts` on the shared `ElectricalNetwork`.

- The system is 28 V DC with a belt-driven 60 A alternator. The alternator control unit (ACU) in the power distribution module (PDM) regulates to about 28.5 V (UND) and has an over-voltage sensor at about 31.75 V (POH NAV III Sec 3).
- The main battery is 24 V, 12.75 Ah, vented "manifold type", on the left forward firewall (equipment list 24-02-R).
- The PDM holds the master (battery) contactor, starter contactor, ACU, external-power relay and the feeder breakers.
- **MASTER** is a red split rocker, ALT (left) and BAT (right). ALT cannot be ON without BAT: a mechanical interlock.
  - Model: `C172Logic` moves the other half on the corresponding edge.
- **Starter**: ignition key START closes the starter contactor.
  - Coil supply is INST (steam) or WARN (G1000). The contactor is `starter` on the PDM.
  - EST 0.065 Ω motor: ~150-250 A cranking current, bus dips to ~16-18 V.
- **Alternator field**: powered from the crossfeed (X-FEED) bus through ALT FLD (steam) or ALT FIELD (G1000).
  - The alternator comes on line with ALT ON, BAT ON and field power.
  - Output vs engine rpm (EST): 0 A below 450 rpm, 36 A at 1000, 54 A at 1600, 60 A from 2000.
  - Model: `maxAmpsVsDrive`, so LOW VOLTS can appear at idle with loads (POH Sec 3 note).
- **ACU over-voltage**: more than 31.75 V for 0.3 s trips the alternator.
  - Model: `C172Logic` then pops the ALT FLD / ALT FIELD breaker (`cb.alt_fld` / `cb.alt_field`), per POH Sec 3.
  - Reset per POH: ALT OFF, breaker in, ALT ON.
  - A runaway regulator (failure `elec.alt.regulator`) applies full field. The alternator then delivers beyond its 60 A rating (EST ~75 A), which pushes the bus over the trip point even with both batteries absorbing charge. See the `drive` expression.
- **External power**: the receptacle is on the left cowl. The PDM relay closes with BAT ON; the external cart also charges the battery. Var `ac.c172.ext_pwr`.
- **Feeders**: the primary buses ELEC BUS 1 and BUS 2 are fed from the PDM through the 60 A FEEDER A/B breakers.
  - The crossfeed bus is diode-fed from both primary buses.
  - The avionics buses are fed through their breakers and the AVIONICS BUS 1 / BUS 2 split rocker (relays).
- **Hour meter**: runs while oil pressure is above 20 psi (the oil pressure switch) and its WARN supply is powered.
- **Clock**: keep-alive from the battery through a PDM fuse.

Bus ids and outputs:

- Bus ids: `batt_bus`, `pdm`, `bus1`, `bus2`, `xfeed`, `avn1`, `avn2`.
- G1000 adds `ess`, `stby_bus`, `lru_pfd`, `lru_adc_ahrs` and `lru_nav1_eng`.
- Outputs are `elec.<bus>_v`, `elec.<bus>_powered` and `elec.<load>_powered`. Also `elec.batt_amps`, `elec.alt_online` and `elec.alt_amps`.

### 6.3 Electrical: G1000 (POH NAV III Fig 7-7, UND)

Breakers (`G1000_BREAKERS`, ratings from UND):

| Bus | Breakers |
|---|---|
| ELEC BUS 1 | FUEL PUMP 5, BCN LT 5, LAND LT 10, CABIN LTS/PWR 5, FLAPS 10, AVN 1 15 |
| ELEC BUS 2 | AVN 2 15, PITOT HEAT 5, NAV LTS 5, TAXI LT 10, STROBE LTS 5, PANEL LTS 5 |
| X-FEED | ALT FIELD 5, WARN 2 |
| ESS | PFD 5, ADC AHRS 10, NAV1 ENG 15, COMM 1 5, STDBY IND LTS 5, STDBY BATT 20 |
| AVN BUS 1 | PFD 5, ADC AHRS 10, NAV1 ENG 15 (the dual-fed units' second feed) |
| AVN BUS 2 | MFD 5, XPNDR 5, NAV 2 5, COMM 2 5, AUDIO 5, AUTOPILOT 5 |

**Essential bus.** Fig 7-7 sheet 2 shows one diode from each primary bus into the ESS bus.

- SCOPE: the model feeds ESS through one diode from the X-FEED bus (itself diode-fed from both primary buses), link `ess_d`.
- The behaviour is identical for every failure except a crossfeed-bus short. It avoids a diode loop, which the network's ideal diodes cannot open. With the loop, the standby battery back-fed the main buses once the MASTER was switched off.

**Dual-fed units.** The PFD, ADC/AHRS and NAV1/ENG (GIA 1 + GEA 71) have identical breakers on AVN BUS 1 and ESS, diode-ORed at the unit.

- Model: a unit bus `lru_*` with two diode links. Only one conducts at a time.
- The ESS feed wins a tie. AVN BUS 1 takes over when it is > 0.5 V higher, or when the ESS breaker is pulled.
- Power vars: `elec.pfd_powered`, `elec.adc_ahrs_powered`, `elec.nav1_eng_powered`, `elec.mfd_powered`, `elec.comm1_powered`, `elec.comm2_powered`, `elec.nav2_powered`, `elec.xpndr_powered`, `elec.audio_powered`, `elec.autopilot_powered`.

**Standby battery.** POH NAV III equipment list 24-07-S: AVT 200413, 14.0 lb. Model: the systems-library preset `BATTERY_172S_STANDBY`, 24 V, 7 Ah (EST: about 30 min at the ~10-12 A essential load; UND gives about 30 min). The STBY BATT switch is ARM / OFF / TEST (momentary).

- **TEST** loads the battery and lights the green TEST lamp while the battery is at or above 23.5 V (EST threshold). The POH preflight test is a 20 s hold with the lamp staying on.
- **ARM with main bus ≥ 21 V**: the battery charges from ESS (link `stby_charge`).
- **ARM with main bus (X-FEED) < 20 V**: the controller releases the battery onto ESS (`stby_discharge`, 20/21 V hysteresis; POH NAV III Sec 3).
- **ARM during the start**: it "helps regulate the ESS bus" (the same path).
- **OFF**: disconnected. It does not charge and does not take over.

**EIS readouts** (NXi PG, UND):

- M BUS volts are measured at the WARN breaker (`ac.c172.m_bus_v`).
- E BUS volts are measured at NAV1 ENG on ESS (`ac.c172.e_bus_v`).
- M BATT and S BATT amps, + = charging (`ac.c172.m_batt_a`, `ac.c172.s_batt_a`).

### 6.4 Fuel (POH Sec 7 Fig 7-6; `systems/fuel.ts`)

The fuel path runs from the two wing tanks by gravity (~0.5 psi head, EST) to the three-position selector LEFT / BOTH / RIGHT. From there it goes to the reservoir tank, the auxiliary electric FUEL PUMP, the FUEL SHUTOFF valve (red push-pull knob at the pedestal base, pull = OFF), the strainer, and the engine-driven pump. It ends at the fuel/air control unit, the flow divider and the injectors.

- Vars: `ac.c172.fuel_selector` (0 LEFT, 1 BOTH, 2 RIGHT), `ac.c172.fuel_shutoff` (1 = ON) and `ac.c172.fuel_pump`.
- The engine gets fuel (`eng1.fuel_on`) with pressure at the servo. That needs the selector and shutoff open, and either the engine-driven pump turning or the aux pump on.
- The aux pump with the mixture RICH primes the engine for the start: 3-5 s, "until stable fuel flow" (POH Sec 4).
- LOW FUEL L/R: below ~5 gal for 60 s (POH Sec 7).
  - The G1000 transmitter-failure case reads −1 gal, which shows as a red X on the NXi EIS.
  - Failures `c172.fuel_xmtr_l/r`.
- Quantity indication power: steam INST (`elec.engine_gauges_powered`); G1000 GEA 71 via NAV1 ENG.
- Engine-driven pump failure `fuel.edp` (= `C172_FAIL.edpFuelPump`): the engine quits until FUEL PUMP ON (POH Sec 3).
- The fuel sump drains (13 points on 172S8001+, POH Sec 4 preflight) are preflight walk-around items. SCOPE: there is no water-contamination model.
- SCOPE:
  - The fuel return line (172S9491+) is not modelled.
  - The vent crossfeed in BOTH is not modelled; both tanks feed equally.
  - The reservoir tank volume is folded into the lines, so the engine quits as soon as the selected tanks are dry.
  - The real airplane runs about 1 min on the reservoir; after FUEL SHUTOFF OFF, the model engine stops within a few seconds.

### 6.5 Engine controls and indication

- **Throttle** `ac.c172.throttle`: 0 = idle (knob out), 1 = full (knob in). Friction lock knob `ac.c172.throttle_friction` (0..1).
- **Mixture** `ac.c172.mixture`: 0 = idle cut-off, 1 = full rich, vernier.
- **Ignition key** `ac.c172.mags`:
  - Positions 0 OFF, 1 R, 2 L, 3 BOTH, 4 START. START is momentary back to BOTH.
  - Key inserted: `ac.c172.key_in`. Removing the key grounds the magnetos.
- **Oil pressure switch**: < 20 psi → OIL PRESS annunciation. Output `ac.c172.oil_press_low`.
- **Tach hours**: `ac.eng1.tach_hours` (the recording tachometer; the steam gauge accumulates it).
- **Hobbs hours**: `ac.c172.hobbs_h`.
- **Failures**:
  - `c172.mag_left` / `c172.mag_right`: dead magneto.
  - `c172.air_filter`: alternate air door opens, ~10 % power loss.

### 6.7 Pitot-static and air data (POH Sec 7, Fig 5-1; `createC172AirData`)

- The heated pitot tube is under the left wing. The static port is on the left fuselage side.
- ALT STATIC AIR valve `ac.c172.alt_static` (1 = pulled ON):
  - It vents the static system to the cabin.
  - The ASI and altimeter read high by the POH Fig 5-1 sheet 2 table (vents/windows closed: +2 to +3 kt).
  - The altimeter also reads high (tens of feet; EST from the same table, checked 5-80 ft at 4000 ft / 100 KIAS).
- **Steam**: `adc1` is the pneumatic ASI/altimeter/VSI source.
- **G1000**: `adc1` is the GDC 74A (digital, powered by `elec.adc_ahrs_powered`). `adc2` is the pneumatic standby ASI/altimeter.
- ASI position error: `C172AsiCalibration` rewrites `adc{n}.ias_kt` from CAS with the flap-dependent POH Fig 5-1 table (flaps 0 / 10 / 30).
- PITOT HEAT is a switch-breaker on BUS 2, about 3.5 A (EST). The heated probe is ice-protection surface `pitot` (IceProtection block).
- The pitot drain hole is modelled: a blocked pitot with the drain open reads 0.

### 6.8 Stall warning (POH Sec 7)

- The warning is a pneumatic reed horn. An inlet in the left wing leading edge is sucked by low pressure near the stall. The horn is above the left door.
- It needs no electrical power.
- Model: `StallWarning` horn on the FDM stall proximity output with threshold 0.4. Output var `ac.c172.stall_horn`.
- Test: the horn onset is 4-11 kt above the break, both flaps up and full flap (POH says 5-10).

### 6.9 Flight controls, trim, flaps (POH Sec 7; `systems/flight.ts`)

- **Controls**: cable-operated, `MechanicalFlightControls`. Pilot inputs `input.pitch/roll/yaw` pass through `C172Logic`.
- **Control lock**: installing it (`ac.c172.control_lock`) pins the wheel with the ailerons neutral and the elevator slightly trailing-edge down (EST −0.12). The rudder stays free.
- **Free play**: 0.2 % (EST). The hardware deadzone is the input layer's.
- **Rigging** (`RIGGING`):
  - Ground-adjustable rudder tab 0.019 (rudder units).
  - Aileron rigging 0.006 (EST).
  - Both are measured as the averages that centre the ball and hold the wings level at 2400 rpm, 90-110 KCAS.
  - A 75 KIAS full-power climb then needs about 1.4 % right rudder and 2 % right aileron, as in the airplane.
- **Elevator trim**: manual trim wheel on the pedestal with a position indicator and TAKEOFF mark.
  - `TrimAxis`, position var `trim.pitch_units` (−1 nose down .. +1 nose up).
  - A cockpit write to the var is treated as wheel movement.
  - TAKEOFF mark = 0.1 (EST: holds about 70 KIAS in the flaps-10 climb).
  - The variant AFCS adds the electric / autopilot trim channels through `createC172Core({ flight: { pitchTrim: { electric, autopilot } } })`.
- **Flaps**: electric, 0/10/20/30 preselect lever `ac.c172.flap_lever` (0..3) with detents at 10 and 20 (POH Sec 7).
  - The motor runs at about 3.5 °/s (EST 0-30° in ~8.5 s) when `elec.flap_motor_powered`: FLAP / FLAPS breaker on BUS 1.
  - With the breaker or power lost, the flaps stay put.

### 6.10 Landing gear, brakes, steering

- **Gear**: fixed tricycle with a tubular spring-steel main gear and an oleo nose strut. Squat legs are the mains.
- **Brakes**: single-disc hydraulic brakes on the mains, from toe-pedal master cylinders.
  - Model: 1000 psi at full pedal (EST), disc temperature model.
  - Parking brake (`ac.c172.parking_brake`): pull the handle and rotate it down. Model: mechanical, holds the pressure.
- **Steering**: nose wheel through the rudder pedals and spring bungee, about ±10° (POH Sec 7). Differential braking tightens turns. The pedals share `input.yaw`.

### 6.11 Lighting (POH Sec 7; `systems/lighting.ts`)

- **Exterior**:
  - Nav lights (wing tips and tail).
  - Flashing beacon on top of the fin.
  - Wing-tip strobes (double flash).
  - Landing and taxi lights in the left wing leading edge. On the NXi these are LED.
  - Wing courtesy lights under the wings, with the dome light.
  - Output `light.<name>` levels; the loads follow them.
- **Interior**:

| Control | Steam | G1000 |
|---|---|---|
| PANEL LT dimmer (`dim_panel`) | Instrument post and internal lights | SW/CB PANELS |
| RADIO LT dimmer (`dim_radio`) | Radios and NAV indicators | AVIONICS (0 = photocell auto) |

Other interior controls:

### 6.12 Cabin environment, doors, ELT, CO

**Heat and air.** CABIN HT and CABIN AIR are push-pull knobs, 0 = in, 1 = out (full).

- The heater air comes from the muffler shroud.
- The left and right defroster knobs are EST controls. Some airplanes have fixed defrost with a single outlet.
- Wing-root and overhead vents: `vent_left/right`.
- Model:
  - Cabin temperature `ac.c172.cabin_temp_c` with a ~2 min time constant (EST).
  - Windshield fog `ac.c172.ws_fog`, cleared by defrost × heat.
  - CO `ac.c172.co_ppm` from failure `c172.muffler_leak` × heat. The G1000 CO LVL HIGH alarm is at 50 ppm (EST).

**Doors and windows.**

- Doors: `door_left/right`, 0 OPEN, 1 CLOSED, 2 LOCKED.
- Storm windows: `window_left/right`, 0..1 (max 163 KIAS open).
- Baggage door: `baggage_door`.

**ELT.** Artex ME406, 406 MHz.

- Steam remote switch: ARM / ON, with TEST.
- G1000 remote switch: ON / AUTO / RESET.
- One var, `ac.c172.elt`: −1 RESET/TEST, 0 ARM/AUTO, 1 ON.
- In ARM/AUTO it transmits (`ac.c172.elt_tx`) after a crash or a longitudinal deceleration beyond 2.3 g (EST G-switch).

**Fire extinguisher.** Portable Halon 1211 between the front seats: `ac.c172.extinguisher` (discharged).

## 7. Normal and emergency procedures

Wording follows the POH, abbreviated where the POH item is an explanation; see `checklists.ts`.

Each list below is implemented with live checks (the `check` functions), and tests cover them.

Normal:

- Preflight cabin and exterior.
- Before starting engine.
- Starting engine (with battery). The G1000 adds STBY BATT TEST 20 s and then ARM, and checks the PFD, E BUS ≥ 24 V, M BUS ≤ 1.5 V, S BATT discharging and the STBY BATT annunciator.
- Before takeoff: run-up at 1800 rpm, mags, vacuum, ammeters, annunciators, idle, friction, flaps 0-10, trim TAKEOFF, parking brake off.
- Normal and short-field takeoff.
- Enroute climb and cruise.
- Descent and before landing.
- Normal landing.
- Balked landing.
- After landing and securing airplane.

Emergency:

- Engine failure during takeoff roll, immediately after takeoff, and in flight (restart).
- Emergency landing without engine power.
- Engine fire in flight.
- Electrical fire in flight.
- Inadvertent icing encounter.
- Static source blockage (ALT STATIC ON).
- G1000 only: HIGH VOLTS / M BATT > 40 A, LOW VOLTS, LOW VACUUM, CO LVL HIGH.
- Steam only: excessive rate of charge, VOLTS annunciator in flight, L VAC / VAC R.

## 9. Initial states (`states.ts`)

| State | Setup |
|---|---|
| `cold_dark` | Everything OFF, key out, control lock installed, parking brake SET, selector BOTH, shutoff ON, mixture idle cut-off, throttle closed, flaps UP, trim at TAKEOFF, STBY BATT OFF. Doors closed, not locked. |
| `ready_to_taxi` | After "Starting engine": ~1000 rpm, mixture leaned for ground ops (best power), MASTER and AVIONICS ON, STBY BATT ARM, beacon ON (nav at night), parking brake SET. |
| `takeoff` | "Before takeoff" complete: flaps 10, trim TAKEOFF, mixture RICH (best power above 3000 ft), strobes/landing/taxi ON, parking brake OFF. |

## 10. Using the shared core (for c172-steam and c172-g1000)

```ts
import {
  C172S_FDM, withEquipment, createC172Core, applyC172State, c172Checklists,
  C172_INPUT_MAP, C172S_META_BASE, createC172Exterior, C172,
} from '../c172s-common';

// fdm: C172S_FDM (steam) or withEquipment(C172S_FDM, extraLb, armIn) (G1000).
const core = createC172Core(ctx, {
  variant: 'g1000',                            // or 'steam'
  flight: { pitchTrim: { electric: {...}, autopilot: {...} } }, // AFCS trim channels (optional)
  electrical: { extraLoads: [...] },           // optional equipment
});
const systems = core.compose({
  sensors: [ahrs, ...], avionics: [g1000 units, radios], afcs: [gfc700], warnings: [...], late: [...],
});
applyState: (s) => { applyC172State(ctx, core, s); /* then radios, AP, displays */ }
checklists: c172Checklists(variant)
inputMap: { ...C172_INPUT_MAP, apToggleEvent: '...' }
exterior: const ext = createC172Exterior(ctx.vars, { ledLights: variant === 'g1000' });
          // ext.root in the AircraftInstance; ext.update(dt) from updateExterior; ext.dispose()
```

- **Power vars for avionics**:
  - Steam: `elec.nav_com1_powered`, `…nav_com2`, `…gps`, `…gyro`, `…xpndr`, `…autopilot`, `…adf`, `…turn_coord`, `…engine_gauges`, and the analog vars in 6.2.
  - G1000: see 6.3.
- **Sensor vars**:
  - `adc1.*`: the steam ASI; the GDC 74A on the G1000.
  - `adc2.*`: G1000 standby.
  - `ac.vac.suction_inhg`, `vac.pump{n}_ok`.
- **Annunciator conditions**: `ac.c172.ann.*`. Steam lamps are `ac.c172.lamp.*`.
- **Failures**: all registered in `core.failures` (building blocks plus `C172_FAIL`).
  - `c172.alt_belt`, `c172.air_filter`, `c172.mag_left/right`, `c172.muffler_leak`, `c172.fuel_xmtr_l/r`.
  - `fuel.edp`, `vac.pump1/2`, `elec.alt`, `elec.alt.regulator`, `elec.batt`, and breaker/bus faults.
- **Rigging**: the core writes `surf.rudder_trim` and `surf.aileron_trim` every frame. There is no cockpit rudder trim in the 172S.
- **Exterior**: `exterior.ts` builds a procedural 172S.
  - Lofted fuselage per station table.
  - Tapered outer wing with dihedral and washout.
  - Struts, horizontal stabilizer with elevators and the right-elevator trim tab, fin/rudder/dorsal.
  - Speed-faired gear with a steering nose wheel.
  - Animated flaps, ailerons, elevators, trim tab, rudder, doors, storm windows, and the prop with a blur disc.
  - Lights: nav, strobes, beacon, landing/taxi spot lights, courtesy.
  - Groups visible from the cockpit (nose, wings, tail, lights) are flagged `visibleFromCockpit`.

## 11. Known gaps / SCOPE

- **ESS diode topology**: one X-FEED → ESS diode instead of two bus diodes (6.3).
- **Fuel**:
  - No return line, no vent crossfeed.
  - Reservoir volume folded into the lines, so the engine stops quickly after fuel starvation.
  - No water or contamination model for the sump drains.
- **Estimated parameters**:
  - Standby battery capacity and TEST threshold.
  - ACU runaway output.
  - Load currents.
  - Cabin temperature, fog and CO dynamics.
  - ELT G-switch threshold.
- **Air data**: flap-dependent ASI position error is post-processed from CAS (both sources). The ground-roll ASI error uses the in-flight table, so it reads low below ~45 KCAS.
- **Handling**:
  - Hands-off at climb power, the airplane turns left: ~10-20° bank after 10 s, bounded.
  - The approach preset is rigged for cruise: slow wing drift hands off.
  - Both behaviours are realistic but tuned only against pilot reports (EST), not flight-test data.
- **Exterior**: checked only in an offline headless-Chromium render of `createC172Exterior` (front, side, top and rear views, surfaces deflected, door open, prop blur). Dimensions agree with POH Fig 1-1 (span 11.0 m, length 8.2 m, fin top 2.68 m). It has not been checked inside the app renderer: the variant agents' visual smoke passes should do that.
- **Out of the core's scope**: the variant agents own the cockpit geometry, instrument rendering, radios, KAP 140 / GFC 700 and the G1000 CAS messages. This dossier's inventory (section 8) is the reference for them.
